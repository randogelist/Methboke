DEFINITIONS

Domain: unforced periodic three-dimensional NSE on the unit torus.
D_inf: actual smooth, periodic, divergence-free, mean-zero physical fields.
physicalEnergy(a) = ||a||_L2^2, without a factor 1/2.
tau_Q(a,nu) = ||a||_L2^4/(8*pi^2*nu^5)
            = physicalEnergy(a)^2/(8*pi^2*nu^5).

D_Q(nu) = {0} union
  {a in D_inf, a != 0 : exists epsilon>0 and actual velocity u, pressure p,
   AnalyticTwoSidedNSECap nu epsilon (2*tau_Q(a,nu)) a u p}.
R_nu(nu) = {a in D_inf : exists U,P, GlobalSmoothNSE nu a U P}.

AnalyticTwoSidedNSECap requires actual joint real analyticity of u,p on
(-epsilon,T) x R^3, periodicity, divergence-free velocity, zero spatial means,
the initial value u(0)=a, and NSE with the ordinary time derivative throughout
that two-sided interval. Its derivative conversion to the original forward
physical derivative is proved, including at time zero.

For nonzero smooth data, the physical L2 representative is proved nonzero;
tau_Q>0 and tau_Q<2*tau_Q are derived. The zero branch uses the explicit zero
solution. No separate positive-cutoff premise is hidden in D_Q.
