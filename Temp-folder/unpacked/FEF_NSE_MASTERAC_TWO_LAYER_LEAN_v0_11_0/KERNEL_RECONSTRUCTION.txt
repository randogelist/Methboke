RECONSTRUCTION NOTES -- v0.7.7

All new proof scripts await the native user compiler. The following describes
the mathematical arguments encoded, not a claim of completed kernel validation.

QUATROS
The finite action is constructed by positive-part/positive-capacity cases.
Square completion bounds every detector, then optimizing bounds the action.
Exclusive survival starts at one and subtracts actual weight*share allocations.
The child/settled/continuing partition proves nonnegative survival and total
ownership <= 1. No common-capacity conclusion is an input field.
The path envelope r(2g+1)<=g+2 is closed under composition. Its polynomial
remainder is 2(g-1)(q-1)(gq-1). At g>=2 it yields 5r<=4.
Finite column sums and the internal gap 121I<=25M give
605M(n+1)<=509M(n)+605P(n). Telescoping retains the exact 96 gap.
The storage proof derives growth from normalized source, debit and half
absorption. Differentiating w(x+A)-charge*V gives a nonincreasing total.
A positive factor floor and the actual debit primitive budget bound A.
Paid cells are A(grid(n+1))-A(grid(n)); their prefixes telescope exactly.
M7 then bounds the actual critical action; the source balance bounds mass
and dissipation; source interpolation gives a single Serrin bound.
The exponential realization of w and physical interpolation are explicit inputs.

MAOTAOTROS
KernelSourceRates supplies derivative/primitive witnesses and local rate
inequalities. KernelCalculus derives the integrated balances, their coupled
sum, source-cell nonnegativity and exact source-prefix identity.
KernelPackets additionally derives local critical absorption from a finite
same-measure source/current/capacity factorization, capacity allocation and
modal energy identity. The full packet/census construction is not yet translated.
CanonicalPaid is max(child-(internal+uv),0). It is nonnegative, routes the
child and is minimal among nonnegative routing payments. Boundary=parent-internal.
The derived 509 step bounds every local excess increment by its paid cell.
Induction and the actual covering grid give excess(t)<=canonicalBudget.
Adding the nonnegative first source cell recovers the source outward bound
Q(t)<=cell(0)+budget+theta*nu^2*D(t). Strict absorption gives critical bounds.
This direct proof is stronger at its intermediate excess bound than the
source potential argument. It does not remove the local-excess or payment inputs.
The entrance fixes the initial readout and viscosity of the same source path.
It contains no critical-bound or signed-production-budget conclusion.

COVARIANCE AND T3
The B15 physical-prefix result is reconstructed from the two original
entrances. A generic contraction gap, not the special 509 coefficient, suffices.
Selected shared-budget and gradient-payment algebra from B13 is also ported.
A periodic endpoint instance is required for B. No R3 route is silently moved
to T3. Finite Fourier integral identities remain the checked baseline;
the stronger unconditional spectral completion proofs remain indexed ports.

INTERNAL VERSUS ANALYTIC INPUTS
