1. SCOPE AND DEFINITIONS

Domain: T^3 = R^3/Z^3; spatial volume = 1; viscosity nu > 0; force = 0.
Velocity and pressure are real-valued and periodic in the spatial variables.
Pressure gauge: integral_T3 p(t,x) dx = 0.
NSE_nu(U,p):
    partial_t U + (U dot grad)U + grad p = nu Delta U,
    div U = 0.
D_inf = C^infinity_{sigma,0}(T^3).
R_nu = {a in D_inf : an actual global classical solution from a exists,
                     smooth up to t=0 on every finite time cylinder}.
FB_nu := forall a in D_inf, a in R_nu.

G_nu(a; epsilon,L):
    epsilon > 0, L > 0, and there exist U,p jointly real analytic on
    (-epsilon,L) x T^3 satisfying NSE_nu there, U(0)=a, and zero spatial mean.
Analyticity is asserted of an actual solution, not merely of a formal jet.

D_germ(nu) := {a in D_inf : exists epsilon>0, G_nu(a;epsilon,epsilon)}.
An asymmetric positive pair of radii defines the same germ class by
restriction to their minimum.

tau_Q(a,nu) := ||a||_2^4 / (8*pi^2*nu^5).
T_Q(a,nu) := 2*tau_Q(a,nu).
D_Q(nu) := {0} union
    {a in D_inf \ {0} : exists epsilon>0, G_nu(a;epsilon,T_Q(a,nu))}.

The zero datum is handled by U=0, p=0; its cutoff is zero.
For nonzero a, tau_Q>0. The finite environment is open at its forward cap:
    E_(epsilon,Q) = (-epsilon,T_Q) x T^3.
The attachment time is chosen strictly inside this environment:
    t_attach = 3*tau_Q/2.
Then tau_Q < t_attach < T_Q, and
    [0,T_Q) union (tau_Q,infinity) = [0,infinity).

By restriction, D_Q(nu) subset D_germ(nu).

3. FINITE ANALYTIC ENVIRONMENT -> QUANTITATIVE COMPARISON BOUNDS

THEOREM FINITE_ANALYTIC_CYLINDER_SERRIN.
Assume G_nu(a;epsilon,L), and 0<T<L. Define
    M_T := max_{(t,x) in [0,T] x T^3} |U(t,x)|,
    G_T := max_{(t,x) in [0,T] x T^3} ||grad U(t,x)||_op.
Then M_T,G_T are finite, U has a strong L2 initial trace a, and
    integral_0^T ||U(t)||_6^4 dt <= T*M_T^4,
    integral_0^T ||grad U(t)||_infinity dt <= T*G_T.

PROOF.
Joint real analyticity implies continuity of U and its spatial first
derivatives on an open neighborhood of the compact cylinder. Compactness
gives the two maxima. Unit spatial volume gives ||U(t)||_6 <= M_T; integration
gives the first estimate. The second follows from the definition of G_T.
Uniform continuity on the compact cylinder gives uniform convergence
U(t,.) -> U(0,.)=a as t -> 0+, hence strong L2 convergence. QED.

Consequently, analyticity through the initial time discharges the finite
Serrin-integrability obligation for each already covered compact interval.
The independent near-zero analytic-admissibility premise in the inspected
positive-time-only uniqueness interface is unnecessary for this stronger
initial-germ hypothesis. The localization required by the originally
inspected interface now has externally verified proofs: a globally admissible
smooth test agrees with the existing front near each compact interval, while
the NSE and pressure premises remain restricted to that interval. Compact-cylinder
continuity yields actual L4-time/L6-space integrability; continuity at zero
yields the original strong physical L2 trace. These exact statements are
included in the accepted package. The two explicit numerical integral
estimates displayed above remain B-tier; in particular the numerical
gradient bound is not promoted by the accepted Serrin-integrability proof.

THEOREM FINITE_ANALYTIC_CYLINDER_IDENTIFICATION.
Assume G_nu(a;epsilon,L), u is a Leray-Hopf solution from a, and
F_RELATIVE_ENERGY. Then u(t)=U(t) in L2 for every 0<=t<L.

PROOF.
Fix 0<T<L. For w=u-U, the divergence-free transport cancellation and the
weak formulation tested against the smooth comparison solution yield
    (1/2)||w(t)||_2^2 + nu*integral_0^t ||grad w||_2^2 ds
      <= integral_0^t integral_T3 |w|^2 ||grad U||_op dx ds.
The initial relative energy is zero. Thus
    E(t) <= 2*integral_0^t g(s)E(s) ds,
    E(t)=||w(t)||_2^2, g(s)=||grad U(s)||_infinity <= G_T.
Integral Gronwall yields E(t)=0. The weakly continuous Leray-Hopf
representative and the continuous comparison give equality at every time
in [0,T], not only almost every time. Since T<L was arbitrary, the stated
identification holds. QED.

The explicit quantitative bound avoids introducing an additional global
analytic-upgrade axiom into this finite-window comparison argument.
The accepted Lean route uses the finite-window Serrin comparison and the
derived L4-time/L6-space bound to obtain the same physical identification.

4. COMPLETE FINITE-CAP CONTINUATION DERIVATION

THEOREM CLAY_ADMISSION_CANDIDATE_01_FINITE_CAP.
Assume F_LH, F_RELATIVE_ENERGY and F_QFB. Then
    D_Q(nu) subset R_nu.

PROOF.
For a=0, use U=0,p=0. Let a!=0 belong to D_Q(nu). Choose its analytic
witness U,p on (-epsilon,T_Q) x T^3 and a global Leray-Hopf trajectory u
from F_LH. Set delta=3*tau_Q/2. Sections 1 and 3 give
    tau_Q < delta < T_Q,
    u(t)=U(t) in L2 for 0<=t<T_Q.
F_QFB supplies a classical tail V,q representing the same trajectory for
t>=delta. On the nonempty open overlap (delta,T_Q), U and V are smooth
and agree almost everywhere in x at each t. Their spatial continuity gives
pointwise equality. NSE then gives grad(p-q)=0; connectedness of the torus
and the mean-zero gauge give p=q on the overlap.
Choose eta in (delta,T_Q). Define the global solution using U,p before eta
and V,q after eta. Equality on an open neighborhood of eta implies smooth
patching of every space and time derivative, preservation of NSE, and
preservation of the initial trace. The initial analytic neighborhood gives
smoothness up to t=0. Hence a belongs to R_nu. QED.

No assumption concerning a trace at the open cap T_Q is required. The
gluing time is strictly smaller than T_Q and strictly larger than tau_Q.
Replacing T_Q by the open cap tau_Q removes this guaranteed overlap;
additional endpoint control would then be necessary.

5. UNIVERSAL INITIAL-GERM ADMISSION IS THE REMAINING ROOT

Define
    COVER_nu := forall a in D_germ(nu), a in D_Q(nu).
This is the exact proposed statement FB^(omega,epsilon->Q)_nu.

THEOREM ANALYTIC_GERM_QFB_REDUCTION.
Under F_LH, F_LOCAL, F_ANALYTIC, F_RELATIVE_ENERGY and F_QFB,
    FB_nu <-> COVER_nu.

PROOF, FORWARD.
Assume FB_nu, and fix a in D_germ(nu). Let W,p_W be its two-sided analytic
germ and V,p_V its global classical solution. Local classical uniqueness
identifies W and V on a positive initial interval. F_ANALYTIC gives joint
analyticity of V for every positive time. Patch the negative-time part of
W to V using equality on the initial overlap and normalized pressure.
This produces an analytic NSE solution on (-epsilon',infinity) for some
epsilon'>0. Restriction to (-epsilon',T_Q) gives a in D_Q when a!=0;
the zero case is included by definition. Hence COVER_nu.

PROOF, REVERSE.
Assume COVER_nu, and fix arbitrary b in D_inf. F_LOCAL supplies a classical
solution v on [0,t_loc). Choose t0 with 0<t0<t_loc. F_ANALYTIC and compactness
of T^3 supply a joint analytic neighborhood of {t0} x T^3. For some
epsilon>0 with (t0-epsilon,t0+epsilon) subset (0,t_loc),
    W(s,x)=v(t0+s,x)
is an analytic NSE germ from a=v(t0). Mean-zero and divergence-free
constraints persist, so a in D_germ(nu). COVER_nu gives a in D_Q(nu).
Section 4 supplies a global classical solution from a. Classical uniqueness
identifies its short initial segment with v(t0+.). Patching gives a global
classical solution from b, smooth at original time zero. Thus FB_nu. QED.

Logical consequence:
    local analytic input restriction alone does not remove the universal
    continuation problem. The universal initial-germ-to-cap bridge has the
    strength of the full periodic smooth-data root under these interfaces.

Scope adapter to the official periodic problem:
Given arbitrary smooth periodic divergence-free a with mean m, put b=a-m.
If v,p solves mean-zero NSE from b, then
    U(t,x)=m+v(t,x-m*t), P(t,x)=p(t,x-m*t)
solves NSE from a. The time derivative contributes -m dot grad v and the
transport contributes +m dot grad v, which cancel. Periodicity and global
smoothness persist. Thus the mean-zero restriction is removable by this
explicit Galilean transform; it is not an additional smallness premise.
The unforced periodic energy identity gives
    d/dt ||U(t)||_2^2 = -2*nu*||grad U(t)||_2^2 <= 0,
so the resulting classical solution also has the uniform energy bound.

6. SPATIAL ANALYTICITY DOES NOT PROVIDE THE INITIAL TIME GERM

For rho>0, let
    a(x)=e1*sum_{k>=1} exp(-rho*k)*cos(2*pi*k*x2),
    U(t,x)=e1*sum_{k>=1} exp(-rho*k-4*pi^2*nu*k^2*t)*cos(2*pi*k*x2).
The nonlinear term is zero because U has only its first component and is
independent of x1. Hence U is an exact global NSE solution with p=0.
The initial datum is spatially analytic. At x=0,
    |partial_t^n U_1(0,0)|
      = (4*pi^2*nu)^n * sum_{k>=1} k^(2*n)*exp(-rho*k).
For integer n>=rho, select k_n=floor(2*n/rho). Then k_n>=n/rho,
exp(-rho*k_n)>=exp(-2*n), and n!<=n^n. Consequently
    (|partial_t^n U_1(0,0)|/n!)^(1/n)
      >= [4*pi^2*nu/(exp(2)*rho^2)]*n -> infinity.
Thus the time-Taylor radius at zero is zero. Differentiation is justified
for every fixed n by exponential summability. Any competing analytic NSE
germ would agree with U at positive times by local uniqueness, hence would
have these same derivatives, a contradiction.

Therefore
    D_germ(nu) proper subset C^omega_{sigma,0}(T^3).
An initial spatial AnalyticOnNhd proof cannot replace a space-time germ
certificate; formal Taylor recursion cannot replace a convergence proof.

7. EXPLICIT UNCONDITIONAL MATHEMATICAL SUBCLASS

THEOREM BELTRAMI_FINITE_FOURIER_ADMISSION.
Let a be a real mean-zero divergence-free trigonometric polynomial and
assume curl a = mu*a for a real constant mu. Then, for every nu>0,
    a in D_Q(nu) and a in R_nu.
No QFB or universal analytic-admission hypothesis is required for this
explicit solution theorem.

PROOF.
The identity curl curl a = grad div a - Delta a gives Delta a=-mu^2*a.
Define, for every real t,
    U(t,x)=exp(-nu*mu^2*t)*a(x),
    p(t,x)=-(1/2)*|U(t,x)|^2
            +(1/2)*integral_T3 |U(t,y)|^2 dy.
Finite Fourier sums and the exponential are jointly real analytic on
R x T^3. Moreover partial_t U=nu Delta U and curl U=mu U. The identity
    (U dot grad)U = grad(|U|^2/2) - U cross curl U
reduces the nonlinear term to grad(|U|^2/2). Therefore
    (U dot grad)U + grad p = 0.
All NSE equations, periodicity, divergence, mean and initial constraints
follow directly. Restrict this entire real-time solution to any finite
analytic environment. The zero case is already included. QED.

Concrete three-parameter family (A,B,C arbitrary real numbers):
    a1 = A*sin(2*pi*x3) + C*cos(2*pi*x2),
    a2 = B*sin(2*pi*x1) + A*cos(2*pi*x3),
    a3 = C*sin(2*pi*x2) + B*cos(2*pi*x1).
Direct differentiation gives curl a=2*pi*a and div a=0. This is the
BAMBUS-compatible ABC/Beltrami family. Arbitrary amplitudes are admitted.
The solution is U(t)=exp(-4*pi^2*nu*t)*a.

This establishes a concrete, nontrivial subclass of the requested admission
class. It does not establish COVER_nu for arbitrary analytic germs.

Small-data reuse has a different scope:
    a in D_germ(nu), represented by c : CriticalSobolev 1,
    CriticalSigmaZero c,
    SingularClosure.criticalNonlinearConstant * ||c|| < nu
      => a in R_nu by the existing small-data endpoint;
      => a in D_Q(nu) by the forward construction in Section 5.
The smallness hypothesis cannot be discarded. The exact norm normalization
and threshold must be those of the inspected Methlib endpoint.
The existing small-data witness is positively smooth and has actual
Leray-Hopf membership. Identify it with the admitted analytic germ near
zero by Section 3 and patch to obtain smoothness up to zero in R_nu.
Apply F_ANALYTIC on its positive-time lifespan and the Section 5 patching
construction to obtain D_Q membership.
