  R3_CANONICAL_CUTOFF_INITIAL_STATE

This candidate constructs the exact carrier-valued initial state `J_n a` from
a literal divergence-free Schwartz datum. It proves the cutoff and Leray
fixed-point laws, compact-test solenoidality and L2 contraction, and attaches
the existing physical H2 and H2-sigma lifts with the same actual velocity and
explicit fixed-cutoff norm control.

It does not define the quadratic convection field, construct a trajectory,
prove local or global existence, prove an energy identity, admit a literal
NSE-A solution on a closed slab, construct the stopped-bound witness, or prove
the R3 obstruction root.
