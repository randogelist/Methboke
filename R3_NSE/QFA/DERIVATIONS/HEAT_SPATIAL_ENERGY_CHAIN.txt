2. FIRST-ORDER INTEGRATION BY PARTS

The accepted compact-test-to-Fourier theorem gives, simultaneously for the
three coordinate directions and almost every frequency xi,

  F(partial_j u)(xi) = m_j(xi) F(u)(xi),
  m_j(xi) = (2*pi*i) * inner_R(xi,e_j).

The multiplier is purely imaginary, so conjugate(m_j)=-m_j. Complex inner
products are conjugate-linear in the first argument. Therefore

  inner_C(x,m_j*y) = -inner_C(m_j*x,y).

  inner_R(V(u),G_j(v)) = -inner_R(G_j(u),V(v)).
