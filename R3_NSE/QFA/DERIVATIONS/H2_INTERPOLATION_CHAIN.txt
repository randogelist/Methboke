1. ACTUAL H2 DERIVATIVES SUPPLY THE SPECTRAL WITNESS

The already proved physicalH2_schwartzDerivativeWitnesses u constructs the
literal distributional identities for complexifyL2 v, complexifyL2 G_j
and complexifyL2 L. Its Laplacian is the diagonal sum of the actual Hessian
and the native tempered-distribution Laplacian. This construction is
discharged from graph membership, not supplied as a new hypothesis.

Applying nativeSpectralRealization_of_schwartzDerivativeWitnesses gives
all three genuine integrability statements and exact spectral identities.
With Fourier convention exp(-2*pi*i*x.xi), let
  q(xi) = |F(complexifyL2 v)(xi)|^2,
  lambda(xi) = (2*pi)^2 |xi|^2.
Then
  integral q = E,
  integral lambda q = Y,
  integral lambda^2 q = Z.                               (1.1)
The equalities identify the original real norms because complexifyL2 is
isometric. Every 2*pi factor remains in lambda.

2. EXACT QUADRATIC-MOMENT ARGUMENT

For every real x, nonnegativity gives
  0 <= integral (x-lambda(xi))^2 q(xi) dxi
     = E*x^2 - 2*Y*x + Z.                                (2.1)
The three integrable moments justify each sum, difference and scalar
multiplication under the integral. The discriminant of this everywhere
nonnegative quadratic is nonpositive:
  (-2*Y)^2 - 4*E*Z <= 0.
This is exactly (0.1).

The accepted theorem

contains this argument. The prepared endpoint composes it with Section 1
and rewrites the complexification norms. No step divides by E,Y or Z;
all zero cases are included automatically.

5. NEXT CONSTRUCTIVE BRIDGES WITHOUT A CIRCULAR ADMISSION

A useful separate target is native simultaneous real Schwartz density in
the PhysicalH1 graph: one sequence converges in velocity and every actual
L2 gradient. Accepted Fourier identities, real Fourier range, Schwartz L2
density and smooth inverse Bessel weights supply a constructive route.
A theorem only giving L2 density of PhysicalH2 velocities is useful for

Once (4.3) has an actual proof, cutoffs, simultaneous H1 convergence and
Fatou can extend it with the same constant. Finite vector/tensor extension
introduces no dimension factor: Minkowski in L3 gives

            <= sum_i ||F_i||_6^2
            <= S6^2 sum_(i,j)||partial_j F_i||_2^2.
This is a bridge after the sharp scalar theorem, not a proof of it.

rearrangement/energy reduction and sharp radial one-dimensional inequality,
or another full sharp Sobolev proof. A norm identity, scalar normalization
or extremizer calculation alone does not prove the all-function estimate.

an assumed construction here. This endpoint constructs no NSE solution,
temporal enstrophy identity, continuation theorem or full QFA root.
