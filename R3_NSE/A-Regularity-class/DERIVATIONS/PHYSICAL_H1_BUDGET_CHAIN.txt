1. ACTUAL PHYSICAL OBSERVABLES

For u in the native complete compact-test PhysicalH1 graph on R3, define
  E(u) = ||velocity(u)||_L2^2,
  Y(u) = sum_{j=0}^2 ||gradient_j(u)||_L2^2.
Both quantities are nonnegative. The graph-coordinate maps are continuous
linear maps, so E and Y are continuous. The native graph norm gives exactly
  ||u||_H1^2 = E(u)+Y(u).
Consequently E(u),Y(u)<=||u||_H1^2 and
  E(u)<=E0, Y(u)<=B -> ||u||_H1^2<=E0+B.
The same implication applies uniformly to any supplied path on any time set.
No whole-space Poincare inequality is required or asserted.

2. FULL CLOSED-SLAB CONTINUITY GIVES A FULL FINITE BUDGET

Let U:R->PhysicalH1 and suppose U is continuous on the actual full closed
finite slab [0,b]. Then t->Y(U(t))^2 is continuous there and thus integrable.
Restriction gives genuine integrability on (0,b). Since the integrand is
nonnegative, the ordinary/extended integral identity is
  J(U,b):=integral^+_{(0,b)} ofReal(Y(U(t))^2) dt
        =ofReal(integral_{(0,b)} Y(U(t))^2 dt)<infinity.
Empty or zero-length windows are covered without an additional assumption.
No derivative or PDE premise is needed for this supplied-path result.

3. FULL FINITE BUDGET PLUS ACTUAL SCALAR EVOLUTION CONTROLS H1

Fix kappa>=0. Assume, explicitly for the same supplied U:
  E(U(t))<=E0 for 0<=t<b;
  Y(U(.)) is absolutely continuous on every [0,R], 0<=R<b;
  d/dt Y(U(t)) <= kappa*Y(U(t))^3 almost everywhere on (0,b);
  J(U,b)<infinity, with the full stopped integral used above.
The independently constructed AC/cubic scalar theorem then yields
  Y(U(t))<=Y(U(0))*exp(kappa*J(U,b).toReal), 0<=t<b.
The explicit full-budget finiteness premise justifies toReal. Combining with
step 1 proves a common preterminal bound on the actual full H1 norm:
  ||U(t)||_H1^2<=E0+Y(U(0))*exp(kappa*J(U,b).toReal).
This is a physical-carrier adaptation of the scalar comparison, with all
energy, differential and budget premises visible in the statement.

4. FIRST REMAINING PDE AND ENDPOINT EDGES

The supplied-path subchains above are complete mathematically. To apply
them to the canonical NSE trajectory, the actual whole-space local/maximal
solution, its energy bound and its AC enstrophy inequality still have to be
constructed. This module contains no continuation or PDE-existence port.
Continuity on every shorter closed interval does not supply the full closed
slab premise in step 2, nor does it establish full-budget finiteness.
The module does not identify the literal Fefferman-A class, provide the
sharp spatial coefficient, or prove universal budget finiteness.
