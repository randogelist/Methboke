1. ACTUAL FREQUENCY CARRIER

Use H=L2(R3;C3) with full Lebesgue volume and Euclidean vector norm. Let C_i
be the six concrete Borel sectors from SIX_SECTORS_CHAIN.txt. For f in H,
1_{C_i} f is strongly measurable almost everywhere, and its pointwise norm
is at most ||f(xi)||. It belongs to L2. Define P_i f to be its actual L2
equivalence class through Mathlib's MemLp.indicator and MemLp.toLp.
This is a construction, with no supplied operator or projection premise.

2. REPRESENTATIVE AND LINEARITY

By the Lp constructor, P_i f(xi)=1_{C_i}(xi) f(xi) almost everywhere.
Indicators distribute over vector addition and complex scalar multiplication;
the a.e. identities prove L2 equality. P_i is complex-linear, and the norm
comparison proves ||P_i f||_2 <= ||f||_2. Package it as a continuous linear map.

3. PROJECTION ALGEBRA

Applying the representative identity twice gives P_i(P_i f)=P_i f. Exact
sector disjointness gives P_i(P_j f)=0 for i!=j. For a finite family, take the
common full-measure intersection of all representative identities. At each
frequency exactly one sector is present, hence sum_i P_i f=f in L2.

4. SELF-ADJOINTNESS, ORTHOGONALITY AND NORM SPLITTING

By the actual L2 inner-product integral, <P_i f,g>=<f,P_i g>: on C_i both
integrands equal <f,g>, and on its complement both vanish. Disjoint sectors
have <P_i f,P_j g>=0. Using idempotence and self-adjointness,
||P_i f||_2^2=<f,P_i f>. Summing and reconstructing yields
sum_i ||P_i f||_2^2=||f||_2^2. All statements hold for complex vector fields,
with no finite-volume assumption or scalar component loss.

5. ACTUAL FOURIER CONJUGATION

Mathlib's L2 Fourier transform F:H->H is a complex-linear isometric
equivalence. Define Q_i f = F^{-1}(P_i(F f)). This gives actual bounded
complex-linear operators. Conjugating step 3 yields Q_i^2=Q_i,
Q_i Q_j=0 for i!=j and sum_i Q_i=I. Plancherel transports the norm bound,
self-adjointness, orthogonality and exact squared-norm decomposition.

6. SUPPLIED MULTIPLIER COMMUTATION

If actual L2 classes f,g satisfy g(xi)=m(xi)f(xi) almost everywhere, their
sector projections satisfy P_i g(xi)=m(xi)P_i f(xi) almost everywhere.
This follows by multiplication of the indicator, without smoothness or
boundedness of m: L2 membership of f,g is already supplied. This is useful
for the coordinate derivative symbols but does not construct a missing
weighted derivative representative.

7. FIRST REMAINING PHYSICAL EDGE / STOP OF THIS CHAIN

The exact complex-vector L2 construction does not by itself prove that Q_i
maps the real-valued physical L2 range into itself. The missing adapter is
Fourier Hermitian symmetry/reflection or an equivalent actual real-range
proof. Only after that and the two-way weak-derivative bridge can these Q_i
be promoted to physical real H1 projectors and charge additivity. Solenoidal
commutation, canonical NSE construction and regularity remain separate.
No parent gap or NSE-A root is closed by this scoped complex L2 package.
