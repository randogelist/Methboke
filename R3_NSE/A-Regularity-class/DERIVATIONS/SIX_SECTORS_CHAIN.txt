1. FIXED PHYSICAL DOMAIN AND EXACT TIE RULE

Let xi in R^3 and a_j = |xi_j| for j=0,1,2. Order coordinates by decreasing
a_j, and let the smaller index win ties. The six permutation sectors are:

C_0 = {a_1 <= a_0 and a_2 <= a_1}       (012)
C_1 = {a_2 <= a_0 and a_1 < a_2}        (021)
C_2 = {a_0 < a_1 and a_2 <= a_0}        (102)
C_3 = {a_2 <= a_1 and a_0 < a_2}        (120)
C_4 = {a_0 < a_2 and a_1 <= a_0}        (201)
C_5 = {a_1 < a_2 and a_0 < a_1}         (210).

In particular, zero lies in C_0 only; there is no omitted boundary or
arbitrary almost-everywhere choice. These match the registered six even cones.

2. BOREL ADMISSION

Each coordinate map xi -> xi_j is continuous on the actual Euclidean carrier.
Absolute value is continuous, and <= comparison is closed while < comparison
is open. Each C_i is a finite intersection of such comparisons, hence Borel.
No smoothness of the discontinuous indicator is asserted or needed.

3. EXHAUSTION

Split first on a_1 <= a_0. If true, split on a_2 <= a_1: the affirmative
case lies in C_0. Otherwise a_1 < a_2; splitting on a_2 <= a_0 gives C_1
or C_4. If a_1 > a_0, split on a_2 <= a_0: this gives C_2. In the remaining
case a_0 < a_2, split on a_2 <= a_1: this gives C_3 or C_5.
Thus every xi lies in at least one C_i.

4. EXACT DISJOINTNESS AND UNIQUE MEMBERSHIP

For each distinct pair of the six listed orders, their inequalities force a
contradiction in the linear order on the a_j. In particular all equalities are
assigned by the lower-index priority and cannot belong to a second sector.
Therefore C_i intersect C_j is empty for i != j. Combined with step 3 this
proves existence and uniqueness of the sector index and union_i C_i = R^3.

5. EVENNESS AND NONTRIVIALITY

The replacement xi -> -xi preserves every |xi_j| and all inequalities.
Consequently -xi in C_i iff xi in C_i. Replace every weak inequality in C_i
by a strict inequality to obtain an open subset. The points (3,2,1),(3,1,2),
(2,3,1),(1,3,2),(2,1,3),(1,2,3), respectively, lie in these open subsets.
Each C_i therefore has nonempty interior; none of the six sectors is empty.

6. ACTUAL SYMBOL ALGEBRA

Define m_i(xi) = 1_{C_i}(xi), a real scalar symbol. Steps 2 and 5 imply
measurability and m_i(-xi)=m_i(xi). As an actual indicator it obeys |m_i|<=1,
m_i^2=m_i. Exact disjointness yields m_i*m_j=0 for i!=j. At every xi exactly
one indicator equals one, so sum_i m_i(xi)=1, including zero and all ties.
