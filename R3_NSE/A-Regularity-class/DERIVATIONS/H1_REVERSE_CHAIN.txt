1. EXACT CARRIERS AND GIVEN DATA

Space is actual Euclidean R3 with full Lebesgue volume. RealL2 and ComplexL2
are actual real-vector and complex-vector L2 equivalence classes. The native
complexification is the already constructed isometric real-linear map.
Let u, du be supplied ComplexL2 fields and e a supplied real direction.
Assume, almost everywhere in frequency,
  F(du)(xi) = (2*pi*i)*<xi,e>*F(u)(xi).
No Laplacian, second derivative, PDE, or norm identity is assumed.

2. FROM AN ACTUAL L2 MULTIPLIER REPRESENTATIVE TO DISTRIBUTIONS

More generally, suppose q,h are ComplexL2 and
  q(x) = c*m(x)*h(x) almost everywhere,
with m real and of temperate growth. Pair with an arbitrary complex Schwartz
test phi. The supplied L2 q has the actual tempered-distribution pairing
  <q,phi> = integral phi(x)*q(x) dx
          = c*integral [m(x)*phi(x)]*h(x) dx.
The product m*phi is Schwartz by temperate growth, hence the last expression
is exactly c times the distributional multiplier applied to h and phi.
Equality on every Schwartz test gives the distribution identity. This uses
the representative hypothesis, not an assumed distribution identity.

3. FOURIER INVERSION RECOVERS THE FIRST DERIVATIVE

For m(xi)=<xi,e>, the native Fourier differentiation theorem identifies the
right side of step 2 with F(partial_e u). Compatibility of L2 Fourier with
tempered distributions identifies its left side with F(du).
Apply the actual inverse Fourier transform on tempered distributions:
  F(du)=F(partial_e u) -> du=partial_e u.
No surjectivity of an assumed gradient record is used.

4. RESTRICT TO LITERAL COMPACT REAL TESTS

For every smooth compactly supported real psi, use its actual Schwartz
representative and complexify it. Evaluation of the distribution identity
and the derivative definition gives
  integral psi*du = - integral (partial_e psi)*u.
Negating and reversing this equality gives precisely the native predicate
  integral (partial_e psi)*u = - integral psi*du.
The real/complex scalar actions agree on ComplexVector. The directional
derivative commutes with scalar complexification by the proved chain rule.

5. REFLECT TO REAL FIELDS BY INJECTIVE COMPLEXIFICATION

Now let u,du be actual RealL2 fields. Before transporting compact-test
integrals, prove both integrands integrable using compact support and L2
local integrability. The accepted integral/complexification identities and
injectivity of the actual real-to-complex isometry reflect the compact-test
equation from complexify(u),complexify(du) to u,du.

6. TWO-WAY DIRECTIONAL AND THREE-COMPONENT CHARACTERIZATION
