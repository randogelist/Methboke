1. INPUT SYMBOL AND ACTUAL FREQUENCY MULTIPLICATION

Let m : R^3 -> R be measurable and even, m(-xi)=m(xi), with a specified
finite nonnegative bound C satisfying |m(xi)|<=C everywhere. These are
properties of an explicitly supplied scalar function, not a multiplier,
reality, derivative, or norm conclusion assumed in a premise.

For an actual complex-vector L2 class f, the representative
xi |-> (m(xi):C) f(xi) is a.e. strongly measurable. Its norm is bounded by
C|f(xi)|. Native MemLp.of_le_mul therefore constructs its L2 class M_m f;
MemLp.toLp supplies the exact a.e. representative identity. Native Lp norm
monotonicity gives ||M_m f||_2<=C||f||_2. Pointwise linearity on a common
full-measure set, followed by Lp.ext, gives an actual bounded complex-linear
map, independent of the chosen representatives.

2. REALITY IS PROVED BY THE ACCEPTED HERMITIAN INVOLUTION

Write Jf(xi)=conj(f(-xi)), using the accepted actual L2 conjugate-reflection
operator. Evenness and reality of m imply

  J(M_m f)(xi) = conj(m(-xi)f(-xi))
              = m(xi)conj(f(-xi)) = M_m(Jf)(xi).

Reflection preserves the full-volume null sets. Thus the displayed
calculation is an equality of actual L2 classes. For a real L2 field u,
the accepted theorem J(F(cu))=F(cu) proves J(M_m F(cu))=M_m F(cu).
The accepted real Fourier-range criterion then proves that F^{-1}M_m F(cu)
is fixed by physical conjugation. Its actual real preimage is constructed
by the already defined realPartL2CLM. Set

  Q_m u := realPartL2CLM(F^{-1}(M_m F(cu))).

The proved range theorem gives c(Q_m u)=F^{-1}M_m F(cu), not merely equality
of a projected real part. Fourier isometry and isometric complexification
give ||Q_m u||_2<=C||u||_2. Actual CLM composition gives linearity. The
Fourier representative of c(Q_m u) is exactly m(xi)F(cu)(xi) a.e.

3. UNIQUENESS AND SYMBOL ALGEBRA

If a supplied real L2 field v has F(cv)(xi)=m(xi)F(cu)(xi) a.e., native
Lp.ext, Fourier injectivity and complexification injectivity prove v=Q_m u.
Consequently identity, composition and equality of scalar symbols may be
proved directly at the representative level. This avoids adding assumed
semigroup, projection or analytic evolution laws to a symbol record.

4. ACTUAL WEAK-DERIVATIVE TRANSPORT

If supplied real L2 fields G_j are the native compact-test weak gradient of
u, their accepted Fourier identity is F(cG_j)=2*pi*i*xi_j F(cu) a.e.

The actual Q_m G_j are L2 fields by step 1. The accepted reverse first-

  SpatialWeakGradient (Q_m u) (j |-> Q_m G_j).

No Schwartz preservation, derivative of m, H2 premise or assumed derivative
