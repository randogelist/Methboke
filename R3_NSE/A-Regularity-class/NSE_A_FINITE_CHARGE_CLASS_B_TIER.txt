PURPOSE
Register the displayed whole-space regularity class
  C_nu^A = {a : L_A(a,nu) < infinity},
with L_A^4 = 4nu J_A, and prove its equivalent characterization by
the full improper integral of six squared enstrophy charges.
The class is defined through the canonical maximal evolution from the datum.
It is not a claim of a terminating initial-data-only membership test.


1. DATA, EQUATION AND CANONICAL EVOLUTION

Fix nu>0 and
  D_A = S_sigma(R^3;R^3)
      = {real Schwartz vector fields a : div a=0}.
All norms and Fourier integrals use full ordinary Lebesgue measure on R^3.
Vector norms are Euclidean; gradient/tensor norms are Frobenius.
No periodicity or mean-zero condition is imposed.

The unforced equations are
  partial_t U + (U . grad)U + grad p = nu Delta U,
  div U = 0,
  U(0)=a.

Let U=U^{a,nu} be the unique canonical maximal H^1 strong/mild solution,
with lifespan T_max(a,nu) in (0,infinity]. On every compact subinterval
of [0,T_max), it is continuous into H^1 and belongs to L^2_t H^2_x.
The trajectory is defined from local theory, before any globality claim.
No value U(T_max) is assumed when T_max is finite.

For Schwartz datum the canonical velocity and normalized pressure are smooth
on every closed finite slab strictly before T_max, including the initial time.
One pressure normalization is
  p = sum_{i,j} R_i R_j(U_i U_j).
The usual freedom is addition of a smooth function of time.

The canonical trajectory and its budget are independent of that pressure gauge.
Strong uniqueness identifies any overlapping canonical strong developments.
No uniqueness assertion is made for unrestricted distributional solutions.

The starting analytic chain is the cumulative finite-window specification [S1],

The present class registration uses:

The precise whole-space Sobolev normalization used in [S1] is

The same constant applies to Euclidean vector/tensor norms by the scalar
inequality for the norm and the corresponding gradient inequality.
Plancherel supplies ||D^2 U||_2=||Delta U||_2 where these derivatives are L^2.

These are established analytic ingredients cited in [S1], not a new

3. THE COMPLETE FINITE-WINDOW BUDGET

Write
  E(t)=||U(t)||_2^2,
  Y(t)=||grad U(t)||_2^2,
  E_0=||a||_2^2.

Define the datum-dependent cutoff
  tau_A(a,nu) = 8||a||_2^4/(27pi^4 nu^5)
             = S_6^6 ||a||_2^4/(2nu^5),
and the finite endpoint
  b_A(a,nu)=min{T_max(a,nu),tau_A(a,nu)}.

For a nonzero datum, both T_max and tau_A are positive, so b_A>0. Define
  J_A(a,nu)
    = lim_{r upward to b_A} integral_0^r Y(t)^2 dt
    = lim_{r upward to b_A} integral_0^r ||grad U(t)||_2^4 dt
    in [0,infinity].

The limit is monotone and retains possible infinity. It is equivalently
the extended nonnegative integral on the entire open interval (0,b_A).
If tau_A<T_max, it is the ordinary integral on [0,tau_A].
If T_max<=tau_A, it includes possible divergence at T_max.
It is not sufficient to have a finite integral on each smaller compact interval.
Local H^1 continuity already gives that weaker statement.

For a=0, use the canonical global zero solution and set
  tau_A=0, b_A=0, J_A=0.
This convention avoids a left-limit notation at a zero endpoint.


4. THE NORMALIZED GAUGE AND REGISTERED CLASS

Define the extended nonnegative quantity
  L_A(a,nu) = (4nu J_A(a,nu))^(1/4),
where the fourth root of infinity is infinity.
Since nu>0,
  L_A^4 = 4nu J_A,
  L_A<infinity iff J_A<infinity.

NSE_A_FINITE_CHARGE_CLASS
  C_nu^A = {a in D_A : L_A(a,nu)<infinity}.

In displayed mathematical notation this is mathfrak C_nu^A.
It is a class of initial data whose membership is determined by their
canonical maximal evolution. L_A is a trajectory-defined gauge.
No triangle inequality, homogeneity under arbitrary scalar multiplication,
linear-space structure, completeness or norm characterization is asserted.


5. FULL FOURIER NORMALIZATION

Use the Fourier convention
  U_hat(t,xi)=integral_R3 exp(-2pi i x.xi) U(t,x) dx,
understood by L^2 extension where the literal integral need not exist.

Define the full Fourier charge
  Q_A[U](t)=integral_R3 |xi|^2 |U_hat(t,xi)|^2 dxi.
The norm inside is the full vector norm. Every frequency participates.
Plancherel gives
  Y(t)=4pi^2 Q_A[U](t).

The WFA Fourier budget of [S1] is therefore
  B_A(a,nu)=lim_{r upward to b_A} integral_0^r Q_A[U](t)^2 dt
           =J_A(a,nu)/(16pi^4).
Set B_A(0,nu)=0. Thus
  L_A(a,nu)^4=64pi^4 nu B_A(a,nu).

The square is outside the entire frequency integral. This is not
integral_time integral_frequency |xi|^4 |U_hat|^4.
Finiteness of the initial spatial charge alone does not give this time budget;
that initial finiteness is already automatic for Schwartz data.

For comparison, on the unit torus the corresponding charge is
  sum_{k in Z^3} |k|^2 |u_hat(k)|^2,
with all vector components and a zero contribution at k=0.
The Laplacian eigenvalue is lambda_k=4pi^2|k|^2. A linear eigenvalue
weight therefore corresponds to a squared wavenumber weight. A weight
|k| instead gives the different homogeneous H^(1/2) squared charge.
No periodic cutoff or mean-zero requirement is transferred to R3.


6. SIX FIXED, REAL-PRESERVING WHOLE-SPACE PROJECTIONS

Fix the coordinate frame once, independently of a, nu, U and time.
For each xi in R^3, sort the three pairs
  (-|xi_1|,1), (-|xi_2|,2), (-|xi_3|,3)
in increasing lexicographic order. Equivalently, sort absolute coordinates
in decreasing order and put the smaller coordinate index first in a tie.

Enumerate the resulting six permutations as
  pi_1=(1,2,3), pi_2=(1,3,2), pi_3=(2,1,3),
  pi_4=(2,3,1), pi_5=(3,1,2), pi_6=(3,2,1).
Let C_alpha contain exactly those xi whose selected permutation is pi_alpha.
The origin belongs to C_1 under this rule.

The sectors C_alpha are Borel: they are finite combinations of comparisons
of continuous absolute-coordinate functions, with the prescribed tie rule.
They are pairwise disjoint and cover R^3 exactly.
Each has nonempty interior, and each is invariant under xi -> -xi.
The tie boundaries have Lebesgue measure zero, but the explicit rule makes
the partition and the multiplier sum exact at every frequency.

For a complex or real vector field f in L^2, define Q_alpha by
  Fourier(Q_alpha f)(xi)=1_{C_alpha}(xi) Fourier(f)(xi).
The bounded multipliers and the L^2 Fourier transform define bounded
linear operators on full-volume L^2.

Projection proof:
  1_{C_alpha}^2=1_{C_alpha}, hence Q_alpha^2=Q_alpha.
  1_{C_alpha}1_{C_beta}=0 for alpha!=beta, hence Q_alpha Q_beta=0.
  sum_alpha 1_{C_alpha}=1, hence sum_alpha Q_alpha=I.
  The indicators are real, so each projection is self-adjoint.
  Plancherel transfers frequency disjointness to L^2 orthogonality.

Real-field proof:
  For real f, Fourier(f)(-xi)=conjugate(Fourier(f)(xi)) almost everywhere.
  Since 1_{C_alpha}(-xi)=1_{C_alpha}(xi), the same identity holds after
  multiplication. Thus Q_alpha preserves real L^2 vector fields.

Derivative and solenoidal proof:
  Each spatial derivative is the Fourier multiplier 2pi i xi_j.
  Scalar multiplier products commute, so for f in H^1,
    partial_j Q_alpha f=Q_alpha partial_j f in L^2.
  The same argument gives the available higher weak derivative identities.
  Multiplication by the scalar indicator preserves xi . Fourier(f)=0;
  consequently each Q_alpha preserves divergence-freeness.
  It also gives ||Q_alpha f||_{H^1}<=||f||_{H^1}.

These operators need not preserve the Schwartz class: the sharp frequency
boundaries need not preserve smoothness of the Fourier transform.
Only their H^1 boundedness and the derivative identities are needed here.
No claim says that Q_alpha U separately solves the nonlinear NSE.

This is a concrete whole-space construction. The periodic six-channel
construction [S3] instead uses coordinate-zero Fourier modes and coordinate
averages. Directly replacing integer frequencies there by continuous
frequencies would collapse those coordinate-zero channels almost everywhere.
The present sectors do not make that domain transfer.


7. SIX CHARGES, EXACT ADDITIVITY AND THE FACTOR SIX

For 0<=t<T_max define
  q_{alpha,A}(t)=||grad Q_alpha U^{a,nu}(t)||_2^2 >=0.
These are six enstrophy charges of the same actual canonical trajectory.
H^1 continuity of U and boundedness of Q_alpha imply continuity of each
charge on compact subintervals of [0,T_max).

By the derivative multiplier identity and Plancherel,
  q_{alpha,A}(t)
    =4pi^2 integral_{C_alpha} |xi|^2 |U_hat(t,xi)|^2 dxi.
The complete partition therefore gives
  sum_{alpha=1}^6 q_{alpha,A}(t)=Y(t).

For any six nonnegative real numbers q_1,...,q_6,
  sum_alpha q_alpha^2 <= (sum_alpha q_alpha)^2
                      <= 6 sum_alpha q_alpha^2.
The first inequality follows by expanding the nonnegative cross terms.
The second is Cauchy-Schwarz against the vector (1,1,1,1,1,1).
Consequently
  sum_alpha q_{alpha,A}(t)^2 <= Y(t)^2
                            <= 6 sum_alpha q_{alpha,A}(t)^2.

For a!=0 define
  J_{6,A}(a,nu)
    =lim_{r upward to b_A} integral_0^r
        sum_{alpha=1}^6 q_{alpha,A}(t)^2 dt
    in [0,infinity],
and set J_{6,A}(0,nu)=0.
Integrate the inequalities on every (0,r), then pass to the increasing
limits. They remain valid when one of the budgets is infinite:
  J_{6,A} <= J_A <= 6 J_{6,A}.
In terms of the normalized gauge,
  4nu J_{6,A} <= L_A^4 <= 24nu J_{6,A}.
There is no claimed equality J_{6,A}=J_A.

NSE_A_SIX_CHARGE_CHARACTERIZATION
  For every nu>0 and a in D_A,
    a in C_nu^A
      iff J_A(a,nu)<infinity
      iff J_{6,A}(a,nu)<infinity.
Equivalently, membership is the finiteness of the displayed full improper
integral of sum_alpha q_{alpha,A}(t)^2 up to b_A.

The same comparison holds for any fixed six orthogonal Fourier projections
resolving the identity and commuting with derivatives on H^1.
The numerical channel budgets may depend on the partition, but their
finiteness class agrees with J_A and is therefore independent of that choice.
The concrete construction above supplies one real-preserving nondegenerate
family without assuming an unconstructed projector witness.


8. FINITE BUDGET EXCLUDES A FINITE ENDPOINT BEFORE THE CUTOFF

This restates E09 of [S1] with all terminal quantifiers retained.
The enstrophy estimate there is
  Y' + nu||Delta U||_2^2 <= pi^(-4)nu^(-3)Y^3.
Gronwall gives, for t<T_max,
  Y(t)<=Y(0) exp[pi^(-4)nu^(-3) integral_0^t Y(r)^2 dr].

Suppose a!=0, J_A<infinity and T_max<=tau_A.
Then b_A=T_max and the COMPLETE improper budget controls the exponent
uniformly as t upward to T_max. The energy estimate bounds ||U(t)||_2.
Thus the H^1 norm stays bounded up to this finite maximal time.
The H^1 continuation theorem extends the solution past T_max, a contradiction.

It follows that
  J_A<infinity implies T_max>tau_A.
This strict conclusion excludes T_max=tau_A by the same argument.
It does not assume a pre-existing regular trace U(T_max).
For a=0 the canonical solution is already global.


9. CROSSING THE CUTOFF GIVES GLOBAL STRONG CONTINUATION

For nonzero a with J_A<infinity, the preceding step makes U available on
the full compact interval [0,tau_A]. The strong energy/product estimate
E06 of [S1] gives
  integral_0^tau_A E(t)Y(t) dt <= E_0^2/(4nu).
Put
  theta=nu^4/S_6^6.
By the definition of tau_A,
  theta*tau_A=E_0^2/(2nu).
Hence a positive-measure set of times in (0,tau_A) satisfies E(t)Y(t)<theta;
in particular choose s strictly inside that interval with this inequality.

The E05 small-product argument applies to the H^1 datum U(s).
Its nonlinear estimate is
  |integral (U . grad)U . Delta U|
    <= S_6^(3/2)(E Y)^(1/4)||Delta U||_2^2.
At the selected time, S_6^(3/2)(E(s)Y(s))^(1/4)<nu.
Energy monotonicity and the resulting positive dissipation margin keep
enstrophy bounded along the restarted strong solution; the standard
continuation criterion then makes that solution global.
This is the invariant small-product bootstrap proved in E05 of [S1].

Strong uniqueness identifies the restarted solution with the original U
on their common interval. Maximality therefore gives T_max=infinity.
No independent trajectory or unproved weak-solution gluing is substituted.


10. CONVERSE AND THE REGISTERED GLOBALITY EQUIVALENCE

If T_max=infinity, H^1 continuity bounds Y on [0,tau_A], and
  J_A <= tau_A * max_{0<=t<=tau_A} Y(t)^2 < infinity
for nonzero a. For zero datum the budget is zero by definition.

Combining this with Sections 7-9 gives:

NSE_A_FINITE_CHARGE_IFF_GLOBAL
  For every nu>0 and a in D_A,
    a in C_nu^A
      iff L_A(a,nu)<infinity
      iff J_A(a,nu)<infinity
      iff J_{6,A}(a,nu)<infinity
      iff B_A(a,nu)<infinity
      iff T_max(a,nu)=infinity.

For a member of the class, the canonical velocity and normalized pressure
are smooth on every finite closed time slab, including t=0, and
  sup_{t>=0}||U(t)||_2^2 <= ||a||_2^2.
This gives the datumwise Fefferman-A conclusion.

For the converse from an arbitrary literal smooth finite-energy Fefferman
pair, retain input A4 of [S1]: on each closed finite slab, the established
bounded-enstrophy and normalized-mild identification theorems identify its
velocity with U. This implies T_max=infinity.
The pressure identification first uses equality of pressure gradients;
no smooth gauge difference is presumed before that identification.
The globality equivalence in the literal category uses this analytic input,
not an already completed Lean adapter.


11. SUBLEVEL CLASSES AND THEIR EXHAUSTION

For each finite R>=0 define
  C_{nu,R}^A = {a in D_A : L_A(a,nu)<=R}
            = {a in D_A : J_A(a,nu)<=R^4/(4nu)}.

Then
  R<=S implies C_{nu,R}^A subseteq C_{nu,S}^A,
and
  C_nu^A = union_{n=1}^infinity C_{nu,n}^A.
The first assertion follows from transitivity of <=.
For the second, every finite nonnegative L_A lies below some positive integer,
and every member of an integer sublevel has a finite gauge.
The zero datum belongs to every sublevel.

The six-charge sublevels
  {a : J_{6,A}(a,nu)<=K}, K>=0 finite,
also exhaust the same class and satisfy the precise comparisons in Section 7.
No equality between equally labelled J_A and J_{6,A} sublevels is inferred.

This is a monotone set-theoretic exhaustion. It is not a compact exhaustion,
a proof of closedness, or a Banach-space construction.
No uniform bound over all admissible data is needed for datumwise finiteness.


12. UNIVERSAL OBLIGATION AND FIRST UNRESOLVED EDGE

For each fixed positive viscosity, the universal assertion is
  C_nu^A = D_A.
For all positive viscosities, this is equivalently
  for every nu>0 and every a in D_A, J_A(a,nu)<infinity,
and also equivalently the corresponding universal six-charge finiteness.
By the established datumwise identification, this is the full global
regularity obligation in the recorded Fefferman-A solution category.
It remains OPEN.

FIRST UNRESOLVED EDGE:
  known energy and weighted-product estimates
    -> finite full J_A for arbitrary Schwartz solenoidal data.
Energy controls integral Y; the stronger weighted estimate controls
integral E Y. The present class requires integral Y^2.
The six-channel inequalities re-express that requirement; they do not
derive it for every datum or remove a potentially singular middle interval.

The initial charge Q_A[a] is finite for every Schwartz datum.
That fact does not prove J_A<infinity and does not make the present class
equal to D_A. The class characterization is an equivalence, not a solution
of the universal regularity problem.
