1. CONVENTIONS AND EXACT SEED

All spatial variables lie in R3. Use the Fourier convention

  Uhat(xi,t) = integral_R3 exp(-2*pi*i*x.xi) U(x,t) dx.

Thus the Fourier symbol of grad is 2*pi*i*xi and that of curl is
2*pi*i*(xi cross). This convention is fixed throughout this document.

Let S = Schwartz(R3; R3). A smooth path into S is understood in its Frechet
topology, not merely as a pointwise smooth function.

For a compact regular time interval J and integers N,r >= 0 define

  q_(N,r;J)(V)
    = max_(j+|alpha| <= r) sup_(t in J, x in R3)
        (1+|x|)^N |partial_t^j partial_x^alpha V(t,x)|.

For a globally time-compact correction, q_(N,r)(V) denotes the same expression
with all real times. Euclidean vector norms are used.

Define the force class

  F_R3^S = C_c^infinity((0,infinity); S).

In words: F is a smooth Schwartz-valued path, supported in one compact
positive-time interval. In particular, for every N,alpha,j,

  sup_(x in R3,t >= 0) (1+|x|+t)^N
      |partial_x^alpha partial_t^j F(x,t)| < infinity.

No common numerical constant across derivative orders is required.

Seed_R3^S(nu,T; U,p,F) means the following.

S1. nu > 0 and 0 < T < infinity.

S2. U and p are real classical smooth fields on R3 x [0,T), with the
    one-sided interpretation at time zero, satisfying

      partial_t U - nu Delta U + (U.grad)U + grad p = F,
      div U = 0.

    The equation at zero may equivalently be obtained by its right limit.
    The initial datum is a = U(0). There is no imposed zero-data condition.

S3. U belongs to C_loc^infinity([0,T); S). Explicitly, on every compact
    preterminal interval, all mixed derivatives and spatial Schwartz
    seminorms are bounded, and time dependence is smooth in these seminorms.
    No uniform Schwartz bound as t tends to T is assumed.

    p is smooth. A separate Schwartz assumption on p is unnecessary:
    the PDE and S2-S4 imply that grad p is Schwartz on regular time slabs.

S4. F belongs to F_R3^S and is defined smoothly through and beyond T.

S5. There is finite preterminal energy and dissipation:

      sup_(0 <= t < T) ||U(t)||_2^2
        + integral_0^T ||grad U(t)||_2^2 dt < infinity.

    These are actual finite integrals, not totalized expressions that
    suppress an integrability premise.

S6. There is one compact spatial set K such that

      for every s in [0,T), every M >= 0,
      there exist t in (s,T), x in K with |U(t,x)| > M.

    This is a compact-local terminal blowup witness. It prevents replacing
    finite-location singular behavior by large values escaping to infinity.

These assumptions do not contain a phase family, a translation-separation
claim, a projected-force-separation claim, or any desired conclusion.

S3 is explicit extra whole-space decay information. Smoothness and finite
energy alone are not being identified with Schwartz regularity.

A seed with one fixed compact spatial carrier for U and smoothness on every
compact preterminal slab satisfies S3. The pinned source in Section 13 has
this property. It also supplies a compact-local blowup witness for S6.


2. EXACT FAMILY STATEMENT

Let

  P(xi) = I - (xi tensor xi)/|xi|^2,    xi != 0,

and let the intrinsic helical projectors be

  Pi_sigma(xi)
    = (1/2) [P(xi) + sigma i (xi cross)/|xi|],
  sigma in {+1,-1}.

The zero-frequency value of these symbols is immaterial for their L2
operators. The physical Leray operator is the L2 Fourier multiplier P.
It is an orthogonal projection and is bounded on every H^m.

Define the complete helical modal power density by

  E_sigma[U](xi,t) = |Pi_sigma(xi) Uhat(xi,t)|^2,    xi != 0.

This formulation requires no globally smooth choice of helical eigenvectors.
In any fixed orthonormal helical frame it equals the corresponding scalar
helical coefficient's squared magnitude.

FDSN_R3 claims

  Seed_R3^S(nu,T; U,p,F)  ==>  Family_R3^S(nu,T; U,p,F).

The exact quantifier order of Family_R3^S is:

  for every s in [0,T),
  there exist a,b,tstar,epsilon, one family U_lambda,
  and one family F_lambda,

  s < a < tstar < b < T, epsilon = pi/4,

such that the following statements hold for the SAME witnesses.

R3.1 — EXACT REALIZATION AND AGREEMENT

  U_0 = U and F_0 = F.
  For every |lambda| < epsilon, the triple (U_lambda,p,F_lambda)
  solves the same-viscosity NSE on [0,T).

  Both corrections

    h_lambda = U_lambda - U,
    G_lambda = F_lambda - F

  belong to C_c^infinity((a,b); S), with temporal support contained
  in one fixed compact subset of (a,b), independent of lambda.

  F_lambda belongs to F_R3^S.
  Initial datum and pressure are identical to the seed.
  Velocity and force agree exactly with the seed outside (a,b).
  In particular, the complete terminal germs are identical.

  The corrections are jointly smooth in parameter, time, and space,
  with smooth Schwartz-valued parameter/time dependence.

R3.2 — COMPLETE HELICAL POWER PRESERVATION

  For all |lambda| < epsilon, all 0 <= t < T, all xi != 0,
  and both helicities,

    E_sigma[U_lambda](xi,t) = E_sigma[U](xi,t).

  There is also rho > 0, independent of lambda and t, such that

    Uhat_lambda(xi,t) = Uhat(xi,t) whenever |xi| < rho.

  Thus the entire low-frequency neighborhood, including the Fourier
  value at zero, is unchanged. There is no arbitrary torus-type spatial
  mean parameter on R3.

R3.3 — TRANSLATION SEPARATION AT ONE COMMON TIME

  For all distinct lambda,eta in (-epsilon,epsilon),

    there is no y in R3 such that
    U_lambda(tstar,x) = U_eta(tstar,x+y) for every x.

R3.4 — PHYSICAL PROJECTED-FORCE SEPARATION

  For all such distinct lambda,eta, there exist t in [0,T) and x in R3 with

    P(F_lambda-F_eta)(t,x) != 0.

  The projected field is interpreted by its smooth physical representative.

R3.5 — WEIGHTED SMOOTH SMALLNESS

  For every N,r >= 0 there is a finite C_(N,r) such that

    q_(N,r)(h_lambda) + q_(N,r)(G_lambda)
      <= C_(N,r) |lambda|,       |lambda| < epsilon.

  The constants may depend on N,r, the seed, the selected interval and the
  cutoffs. No constant uniform across all derivative orders is asserted.

Every family member also retains the same energy and dissipation histories
and the same compact-local blowup witness. These facts are proved below.


3. A NONZERO CONTINUOUS-FREQUENCY PATCH

Fix s in [0,T).

By S6, choose tstar in (s,T) such that U(tstar) is not the zero field.
Fourier injectivity on S implies Uhat(tstar) is not identically zero.

There exist a coordinate j and a frequency xi0 != 0 for which

  Uhat_j(xi0,tstar) != 0.

Indeed, a continuous function supported only at the point zero would be
identically zero. By continuity, choose an open ball V around xi0, with
compact closure avoiding zero, such that

  V cap (-V) = empty,
  Uhat_j(xi,tstar) != 0 for every xi in V.

The nonzero condition can be made quantitatively uniform on a smaller
closed ball if desired.

Choose two open balls A and B with disjoint compact closures contained in V.

  A = active patch.
  B = untouched anchor patch.

No triad relation or nonlinear interaction condition is required here.
The nonzero open frequency region is supplied by Fourier continuity of
a nonzero Schwartz field.


4. AN ODD PHASE WITH ACTIVE AND UNTOUCHED PATCHES

Choose chi in C_c^infinity(V; R) such that

  0 <= chi <= 1,
  chi = 1 on a neighborhood of closure(A),
  chi = 0 on a neighborhood of closure(B).

Arrange the support of chi to avoid closure(B), using a slightly larger
neighborhood of closure(A) still disjoint from closure(B) and contained in V.

Define

  psi(xi) = chi(xi) - chi(-xi).

Then:

  psi is real, smooth, compactly supported away from zero;
  psi(-xi) = -psi(xi);
  |psi| <= 1;
  psi = 1 on A;
  psi = 0 on B.

The support is contained in V union (-V), so psi vanishes in some ball
around frequency zero.

Choose

  a = (s+tstar)/2,
  b = (tstar+T)/2.

Then 0 < a < tstar < b < T and s < a.

Choose a real normalized time bump

  beta in C_c^infinity((a,b)),
  0 <= beta <= 1,
  beta(tstar) = 1.

Let K_beta = supp beta, a fixed compact subset of (a,b).


5. EXPLICIT VELOCITY AND FORCE

For every real lambda, and 0 <= t < T, define

  m_lambda(t,xi) = exp(i lambda beta(t) psi(xi)),

  Uhat_lambda(xi,t) = m_lambda(t,xi) Uhat(xi,t).

For the theorem restrict |lambda| < epsilon = pi/4.

Equivalently, set

  h_lambda(t)
    = inverseFourier[(exp(i lambda beta(t) psi)-1) Uhat(t)]

on the regular slab and define h_lambda = 0 outside that slab.

This definition is unambiguous because the multiplier increment vanishes
outside K_beta. In particular no value of the original singular U at or
after T is used.

Set U_lambda = U + h_lambda for preterminal times.

Keep the pressure unchanged:

  p_lambda = p.

On the regular slab define

  G_lambda =
      partial_t h_lambda - nu Delta h_lambda
      + (U.grad)h_lambda
      + (h_lambda.grad)U
      + (h_lambda.grad)h_lambda,

and extend G_lambda by zero outside that slab.

Define F_lambda = F + G_lambda for all real times.

All constructions use the same psi,beta,a,b,tstar for every lambda.


6. SCHWARTZ REGULARITY, GLOBAL TIME EXTENSION, AND SMALLNESS

Let

  q_lambda(t,xi)
    = (exp(i lambda beta(t) psi(xi))-1) Uhat(t,xi).

Its frequency support is contained in the fixed compact set supp psi.

On a compact regular time interval containing K_beta, Uhat is jointly
smooth in time and frequency with all required derivatives bounded on
supp psi. The scalar multiplier is smooth in lambda and equals one at
lambda zero.

The fundamental theorem of calculus in lambda gives

  q_lambda = integral_0^lambda partial_theta q_theta dtheta.

Hence for every j and frequency multiindex gamma,

  sup_(t,xi) |partial_t^j partial_xi^gamma q_lambda|
    <= C_(j,gamma) |lambda|,    |lambda| <= pi/4,

where the supremum is over the fixed compact support sets.

For spatial multiindices alpha,gamma, Fourier inversion and integration
by parts bound

  sup_x |x^gamma partial_x^alpha partial_t^j h_lambda|
    <= C_(alpha,gamma)
       ||partial_xi^gamma
           [xi^alpha partial_t^j q_lambda]||_L1(xi).

The right side is O(|lambda|), because its support is fixed and compact.
Finite sums of monomial weights control (1+|x|)^N. Thus

  q_(N,r)(h_lambda) <= C_(N,r) |lambda|.

The same argument with parameter derivatives proves joint smooth
Schwartz-valued dependence on (lambda,t).

Since K_beta is strictly inside (a,b), h_lambda vanishes in neighborhoods
of both slab boundaries. Its zero extension is globally smooth and has
temporal support in K_beta.

All derivatives of U are Schwartz-bounded on a slightly larger compact
regular slab. Differentiation and multiplication are continuous on S.
Leibniz's rule therefore proves

  G_lambda in C_c^infinity((a,b); S).

The linear terms in G_lambda are O(|lambda|); its quadratic term is
O(lambda^2). On the fixed parameter interval |lambda| <= pi/4, these yield

  q_(N,r)(G_lambda) <= C'_(N,r) |lambda|.

One may equivalently differentiate G_lambda in lambda on the compact
parameter interval and apply the fundamental theorem of calculus,
using G_0 = 0.

This proves R3.5 and all joint regularity claims in R3.1.

As F and G_lambda are both Schwartz-valued and supported in compact
positive-time intervals, their sum belongs to F_R3^S. Every weighted
spacetime derivative of F_lambda satisfies the rapid-decay force bounds.


7. ACTUAL NSE, REALITY, DIVERGENCE, AND BOUNDARY HISTORIES

REALITY

The seed is real, so Uhat(-xi,t) = conjugate(Uhat(xi,t)).
Oddness of psi gives

  m_lambda(t,-xi) = conjugate(m_lambda(t,xi)).

Thus Uhat_lambda retains Fourier conjugacy and U_lambda is real.

DIVERGENCE

The seed satisfies xi.Uhat(xi,t) = 0. Since m_lambda is scalar,

  xi.Uhat_lambda(xi,t) = m_lambda(t,xi) xi.Uhat(xi,t) = 0.

Fourier inversion gives div U_lambda = 0.

MOMENTUM EQUATION

Expand U_lambda = U + h_lambda:

  (U_lambda.grad)U_lambda
    = (U.grad)U + (U.grad)h_lambda
      + (h_lambda.grad)U + (h_lambda.grad)h_lambda.

Subtract the seed equation. The remaining terms are exactly G_lambda
as defined in Section 5. Therefore

  partial_t U_lambda - nu Delta U_lambda
    + (U_lambda.grad)U_lambda + grad p = F_lambda.

This is the actual differential equation with unchanged pressure.

INITIAL AND FINAL HISTORIES

Because K_beta is compactly contained in (a,b),

  U_lambda(t) = U(t) for every preterminal t outside (a,b),
  F_lambda(t) = F(t) for every real t outside (a,b).

In particular U_lambda(0) = U(0), and the complete velocity/force germs
near T are identical. No extension of U through the blowup time has been
used to define either correction.

This proves the remaining assertions of R3.1.


8. FULL HELICAL POWERS AND LOW FREQUENCIES

For xi != 0 the real transverse projector P and the Hermitian operator

  J(xi) = i (xi cross)/|xi|

satisfy

  J^2 = P,  PJ = JP = J.

Consequently Pi_sigma = (P + sigma J)/2 are mutually orthogonal Hermitian
projections onto the two curl-helicity lines.

Since the multiplier is scalar,

  Pi_sigma(xi) Uhat_lambda(xi,t)
    = m_lambda(t,xi) Pi_sigma(xi) Uhat(xi,t).

Its modulus is one. Therefore

  |Pi_sigma Uhat_lambda|^2 = |Pi_sigma Uhat|^2

for every nonzero frequency, both helicities, and every preterminal time.

This proves R3.2. It is exact pointwise equality of smooth Fourier data,
not merely equality of shell integrals.

The construction is even independent of the helical observation: every
fixed complex-linear observation of a vector Fourier coefficient receives
the same scalar phase and therefore preserves its magnitude.

As psi vanishes near zero, Uhat_lambda equals Uhat on a fixed neighborhood
of zero for every preterminal time.

For an integrable divergence-free R3 field, Uhat(0)=0: take xi=r e in
xi.Uhat(xi)=0, divide by r, and pass to zero for every unit direction e.
Thus no arbitrary nonzero spatial-mean parameter is imported from T3.


9. SEPARATION MODULO EVERY SPATIAL TRANSLATION

Suppose for parameters lambda,eta in (-pi/4,pi/4) and y in R3,

  U_lambda(tstar,x) = U_eta(tstar,x+y) for every x.

The Fourier translation formula gives

  m_lambda(tstar,xi) Uhat(xi,tstar)
    = exp(2*pi*i*xi.y)
      m_eta(tstar,xi) Uhat(xi,tstar).

On the untouched open ball B, psi=0 and the selected component Uhat_j
is nonzero. Therefore

  exp(2*pi*i*xi.y) = 1 for every xi in B.

The continuous linear function xi -> xi.y consequently takes integer
values on an open ball, so is constant there. It follows that y=0.
Equivalently, differentiating the character on B gives every component
of y equal to zero.

On the active ball A, psi=1 and beta(tstar)=1. With y=0, the same component
identity becomes

  exp(i lambda) Uhat_j(xi,tstar)
    = exp(i eta) Uhat_j(xi,tstar).

Hence exp(i(lambda-eta))=1. But |lambda-eta|<pi/2, so lambda=eta.

This proves R3.3 by contradiction whenever lambda != eta.

The continuum of untouched frequencies removes every translation freedom.
The torus active-triad certificate is unnecessary for this R3 construction.


10. PHYSICAL LERAY-FORCE SEPARATION

Fix distinct admitted parameters lambda,eta. Put

  u = U_lambda,  v = U_eta,  w = u-v,
  deltaF = F_lambda-F_eta.

Assume for contradiction that P(deltaF)=0 throughout the preterminal
interval. We only need this assumption on the compact slab [a,tstar].

The two velocities agree at a, since their temporal corrections vanish
in a neighborhood of a. Therefore w(a)=0.

Subtract their NSE equations. Their pressures are identical, so the
pressure terms cancel. Write

  (u.grad)u - (v.grad)v = (u.grad)w + (w.grad)v.

Take the L2 inner product with w. The fields on this compact time slab
have all the required decay and regularity. Incompressibility and
integration by parts eliminate the transport contribution:

  integral ((u.grad)w).w = 0.

Orthogonality of the Leray projection, and div w=0, give

  integral deltaF.w = integral P(deltaF).w = 0.

Therefore

  (1/2) d_t ||w||_2^2 + nu ||grad w||_2^2
    <= ||grad v||_infinity ||w||_2^2.

The coefficient ||grad v||_infinity is bounded on [a,tstar].
Gronwall and w(a)=0 imply w(tstar)=0.

This contradicts R3.3 with the zero translation. Therefore there is a
preterminal time at which P(deltaF) is nonzero in L2.

For each time, deltaF is Schwartz. Since the Leray symbol is bounded,
P(deltaF) lies in every H^m. Sobolev embedding gives a smooth physical
representative; it cannot be pointwise zero everywhere if its L2 class is
nonzero. Hence there exist t<T and x with

  P(F_lambda-F_eta)(t,x) != 0.

This proves R3.4.

No assertion that the Leray projection of a Schwartz force is Schwartz
is needed: its low-frequency symbol need not preserve that space.
The physical forces F_lambda themselves are Schwartz, and the projected
force has the smooth representative just established.


11. ENERGY, DISSIPATION, AND TERMINAL SINGULARITY

The multiplier has unit modulus at every frequency. Plancherel therefore
gives, for every preterminal t,

  ||U_lambda(t)||_2^2 = ||U(t)||_2^2,
  ||grad U_lambda(t)||_2^2 = ||grad U(t)||_2^2.

The energy bound and finite integrated dissipation are retained exactly.
For every shorter interval, and for the full interval (0,T),

  integral ||grad U_lambda(t)||_2^2 dt
    = integral ||grad U(t)||_2^2 dt.

For terminal blowup, let K be the compact witness from S6. Given s<T and
M>=0, apply S6 after max(s,b). The resulting t belongs to (b,T), where
U_lambda=U. Thus the same point x in K witnesses |U_lambda(t,x)|>M.

This is exact preservation of the compact-local terminal singularity.
The family does not merely preserve a numerical supremum over all space.

All members satisfy the same seed-class energy, dissipation, and blowup
requirements. The family is therefore a family of admitted singular
histories, with different effective forcing histories.


12. FURTHER EXACT QUADRATIC IDENTITIES — B-TIER COROLLARIES

For every real r and every preterminal time,

  ||U_lambda(t)||_(H^r) = ||U(t)||_(H^r).

All inhomogeneous H^r norms are finite for a Schwartz slice. Homogeneous
norms agree whenever finite, or as extended nonnegative Fourier integrals;
no blanket finiteness of all negative homogeneous orders is asserted.

Helicity is also identical, since with the fixed 2*pi Fourier convention,

  integral_R3 U.curl U
    = integral_R3 2*pi*|xi| (E_+(xi)-E_-(xi)) dxi.

Energy, enstrophy, helicity, and dissipation histories therefore agree.

The instantaneous total force work agrees as well. On each regular time
slice, the classical energy identity gives

  integral_R3 F_lambda.U_lambda
    = (1/2) d_t ||U_lambda||_2^2
      + nu ||grad U_lambda||_2^2
    = integral_R3 F.U.

The pressure contribution vanishes because grad p belongs to L2 by the
PDE and is orthogonal to solenoidal Schwartz velocities. These statements
are ordinary mathematical consequences of the construction, not newly
audited Lean declarations.

The force norms and complete residual-force observations need not agree.
The theorem has not produced a regular/singular pair with identical
spectra: every member retains the same exact singular tail.

14. BOUNDARIES AND FIRST UNRESOLVED EDGES FOR STRONGER VARIANTS

14.1 Literal single-frequency rotation does not port.

On R3, changing an L2 Fourier transform only at {xi0,-xi0} changes it
on a measure-zero set and hence does not change the physical L2 field.
The present construction uses smooth frequency regions of positive
measure. This resolves the literal-port problem rather than assuming
a nonzero effect from isolated frequencies.

14.2 Compact spatial support is not preserved.

The correction h_lambda has compact Fourier support. At tstar it is
nonzero for lambda != 0. Such a function cannot also have compact
spatial support: its inverse Fourier integral extends analytically,
and vanishing on the exterior open set would force it to be zero.

Therefore the output guarantee is precisely

  h_lambda, G_lambda in C_c^infinity((a,b); S),

not compact spacetime support. The proof gives no general preservation
of F_lambda in C_c^infinity(R3 x (0,infinity)).

The stronger compact-spatial-force variant is left unresolved. Its
support-admission edge is not promoted by the rapid-decay theorem.

14.3 Bare smooth finite-energy seeds are insufficient for this proof.

For a general L2 seed, Uhat need only be an L2 function. A compact
frequency cutoff makes its inverse transform smooth but does not,
without frequency derivatives, make it Schwartz. Even all unweighted
Sobolev norms do not by themselves supply spatial moments.

The first unresolved edge for the broader theorem is

  smooth finite-energy seed
    --> required frequency regularity
    --> Schwartz correction and rapid-decay force admission.

The derivation of that broader variant stops at this edge. The present
complete theorem retains S3 explicitly.

A weaker sufficient premise could replace S3 by smoothness of Uhat on
the selected compact frequency region together with polynomial bounds
for all physical derivatives of U on the relevant time slab. Admission
of those premises for an unrestricted seed is not established here.

14.4 Global sup blowup alone does not locate a singularity.

On an unbounded domain, large values can move to infinity. S6 uses one
compact K, and the pinned seed has a fixed compact carrier. The stronger
local interpretation is therefore explicit.

14.5 Force variation and regularity classification.

The family changes its physical effective force. It does not assert
fixed-force nonuniqueness or an unforced analogue. All members share
the terminal singularity. The result does not rule out every possible
spectral regularity criterion and does not supply a regular/singular
indistinguishability pair.

17. SIGNED TIME-PHASE REFINEMENT AND DIRECT PROJECTED-FORCE SEPARATION

This cumulative refinement preserves the exact Seed, Family, parameter
interval, common comparison time tstar, and all five required conclusions.
It replaces the difference-energy/Gronwall method for force separation by
an explicit common-time witness. The nonnegative bump remains a building
block; the final time phase is allowed to change sign. Family never
required that phase itself to be nonnegative or bounded by one.

17.1 Selection without a strengthened seed

Retain the tstar, nonzero Fourier component i, active center c, untouched
patch, and odd frequency cutoff psi from Sections3-4. In particular,
psi(c)=1 and Uhat_i(tstar,c) is nonzero. Let rho be the normalized smooth
compact interior bump from Section4: rho(tstar)=1, and its support lies
in (a,b).

The original local-Schwartz hypothesis implies continuity of
  t -> Uhat_i(t,c)
by dominated convergence on a compact regular slab. Indeed its zero-order
weighted bound with an integrable inverse-polynomial spatial tail supplies
one integrable dominating function. No terminal-uniform bound is used.
Continuity of this coefficient and rho supplies a neighborhood of tstar
on which both are nonzero. Choose a distinct t0 in that neighborhood and
in (a,b), for example on its left side.

Define the signed time phase

  beta(t) = ((t-t0)/(tstar-t0)) rho(t).

Then beta is smooth and has the same compact temporal carrier as a subset
of rho's carrier. Direct differentiation gives

  beta(tstar)=1,
  beta(t0)=0,
  beta'(t0)=rho(t0)/(tstar-t0) != 0.

The denominator is nonzero by t0 != tstar. All derivatives of beta are
bounded on its compact carrier. The estimates of Section6 hold with these
finite bounds absorbed into their constants. Modulus-one phase identities
do not require a sign or unit bound on beta. Translation separation still
uses beta(tstar)=1 and the original active and untouched patches.

17.2 The actual real solenoidal Schwartz generator

Use the same inverse-Fourier family and residual as before, now with this
beta. Set

  W(x) = inverseFourier[ i psi(xi) Uhat(t0,xi) ](x).

The spectral function is smooth and compactly supported, hence Schwartz.
Its Hermitian symmetry follows from reality of U and oddness of psi:

  (i psi Uhat)(-xi) = conjugate[(i psi Uhat)(xi)].

Consequently W is a real Schwartz vector field. Since div U(t0)=0,
xi dot Uhat(t0,xi)=0, and scalar multiplication retains this identity.
Fourier inversion therefore gives div W=0.

At c its i-th Fourier component is

  What_i(c) = i Uhat_i(t0,c) != 0.

Fourier injectivity gives W != 0, so some physical x0 has W(x0) != 0.
For this actual Schwartz field, the transverse symbol acts identically
at every frequency. Thus the literal inverse-Fourier physical Leray field
satisfies P W = W; no abstract assumed projection witness is introduced.

17.3 Exact force difference at one common time

At t0, beta(t0)=0 implies U_lambda(t0)=U(t0) for every lambda.
Thus h_lambda(t0)=0 as an entire spatial field; every spatial derivative
of that zero slice is zero. The viscous and every nonlinear residual term
vanish at t0.

Differentiating the actual inverse-Fourier correction is justified by the
compact spectral carrier and the original local-Schwartz time regularity.
The derivative of Uhat is multiplied by the zero phase increment. The
remaining term is exactly

  partial_t h_lambda(t0) = lambda beta'(t0) W.

Consequently

  F_lambda(t0)-F_eta(t0) = (lambda-eta) beta'(t0) W.

This difference is itself a real solenoidal Schwartz field, so its physical
Leray projection equals the same expression. For lambda != eta and the
physical point x0 selected above,

  P(F_lambda-F_eta)(t0,x0)
    = (lambda-eta) beta'(t0) W(x0) != 0.

This proves the required physical projected-force separation directly,
with one common t0 and one common nonzero generator for all parameter
pairs. The original same-witness theorem requires only existence of a
spacetime witness for each pair, so this conclusion is stronger.
