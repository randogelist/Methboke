# What the existing identities do not yet prove

The cutouts are reusable foundations. The nine-module obstruction still needs these new arguments:

1. Literal canonical reciprocal definitions, sign/normalization and the chain-rule composition.
2. A guarded-to-interior rate argument and the single-cutoff mean-value contradiction; preserve the original stopped premise.
3. Actual-reference compact Sobolev/C1 control on a closed subslab with T<S.
4. Componentwise Fourier C1 estimates, cutoff-complement tail symbols and physical/Fourier norm conversions.
5. The actual reference's Fourier coefficient evolution and the finite forced-error coefficient equation.
6. Differentiation of the finite error enstrophy sum, with the correct projection and physical pairing. The error w does not solve the original unforced NSE.
7. Mixed-convection and residual L2 estimates, viscosity allocation, derivative-to-right-slope adaptation, and the H1 error bootstrap.
8. Eventual uniform enstrophy from that actual front, and assembly of the unchanged conditional root.

The trajectory-specific MLB11 identity is useful context; AUX01 is its arbitrary-coefficient counterpart for error fields. The signed slope Gronwall source ML03 requires an adapter; an absolute-derivative norm inequality is stronger and cannot be assumed merely to use a neighboring theorem.

The full operator interpretation separately needs the real phase adapter, complete flow, half-density group, actual generator/domain/adjoint proof, graph core and canonical localization. Native LinearPMap and TestFunction declarations do not discharge those constructions.

No identity here establishes the stopped-bound witness. No new analytic hypothesis, global axiom, or formal acceptance is introduced by this collection.
