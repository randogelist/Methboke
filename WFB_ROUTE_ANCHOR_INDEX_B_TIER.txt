1. EXACT SCOPE AND EXISTING ACCEPTANCE

This is a B-tier index of the remaining formalization route, not a newly
compiled WFB proof. Weak_Fourierman_B_B_TIER.txt retains the mathematical
composition relative to its explicitly cited classical periodic local theory
and indexed QFB results. The present source-only route stops at the missing
formal physical local-existence edge. The later entries inventory available
tools and exact remaining contracts; they are not a completed implication chain.

Fixed R0 remains accepted: an actual initial classical NSE cap [0,T) with
nu>0 and tau_Q(a,nu)<T gives a GlobalSmoothNSE pair preserving the full cap's
velocity and normalized pressure. The analytic D_Q subset R_nu wrapper is
also accepted. Universal germ-to-cap R1 remains open.

WFB-01 and WFB-02 remain the only accepted whole WFB contextual obligations.
Accepted comparison, pressure and gluing helpers substantially reduce the
remaining work but do not by themselves promote WFB-03 through WFB-10.
Route labels R01--R10 below are work packages, not replacement obligation IDs.

The intended WFB statement retains its finite-budget hypothesis:
  nu>0; a smooth, periodic, divergence-free and mean-zero on the unit torus;
  (u,p) the actual unique maximal strong trajectory, with 0<Tmax<=infinity;
  tau_Q = ||a||_2^4/(8*pi^2*nu^5);
  Q(t) = sum_(k!=0) |k|^2 |u_hat_k(t)|^2;
  integral_(0,min(Tmax,tau_Q)) Q(t)^2 dt < infinity
    ==> Tmax=infinity and a global smooth pair agreeing with the entire
        original velocity and normalized pressure.

The initial sum/integral are nonnegative extended operations. The full open
interval budget is required, not only separate compact-interval finiteness.
Unconditional formal WFB means the implication has no additional unproved
local-existence/continuation axiom. It does not mean removing its budget.

4. EXACT NORMALIZATIONS AND EDGE CASES

Define tau=(min Tmax (ENNReal.ofReal tau_Q)).toReal after proving this
minimum finite, since it is bounded by ofReal tau_Q. Use 0<=b<tau.
The R05/R07 closed-slab estimates require InitialClassicalFront smoothness
through zero; positive-time smoothness with only an L2 trace is insufficient.

E(t)=physicalEnergy(u(t))=||u(t)||_2^2; no factor 1/2.
Y(t)=physicalDissipation(u(t))=||grad u(t)||_2^2=4*pi^2*Q(t).
Z(t)=||Delta u(t)||_2^2. Thus integral Y^2=16*pi^4*integral Q^2.
The scalar CubicBudget theorem uses nu*z(t)^2, so choose z=||Delta u||_2,
not z=Z. It requires continuous y,z,d on each closed slab [0,b], the actual
derivative on (0,b), and d+nu*z^2<=kappa*y^3. The explicit enstrophy formula
must supply continuous d. Choose b<Tmax and use one bound M from the full
budget; never assume regularity, continuity or a value at finite Tmax.

Then the prospective R07 conclusion is
  E(t)+Y(t) <= E(0)+Y(0)*exp(kappa*M),  0<=t<Tmax,
when the budget covers the whole possibly finite maximal interval.
Take R as the square root of the right side in the physical H1 norm convention
E+Y, or prove the equivalence constant for any Fourier-weighted H1 convention.
CriticalSobolev q in Methlib denotes H^(q/2), not H^q.

R01 need only be uniform on smooth data with this H1 bound. A full rough-H1
local theory suffices, but is not a necessary extra target for this route.
The required duration is independent of N, higher derivatives and start time.
R01 and R02 can be one physical construction presented as two review steps.
R02 may not assume smoothness/pressure by putting them into an uninhabited
certificate structure. Once InitialClassicalFront is constructed, its strong
initial L2 trace is already derived by the accepted adapter.

If Tmax<=tau_Q, the full budget is exactly on (0,Tmax). Uniform restart rules
out this case, including Tmax=tau_Q. Q09 alone cannot rule out equality because
it applies strictly after the cutoff. Once tau_Q<Tmax, a real finite T between
them gives the R0 input. Neither 2*tau_Q<Tmax nor analyticity is required.

If Tmax=infinity, select finite cap times without taking its real value.
Taking min(Tmax,ofReal(tau_Q)) first is finite; arbitrary use of toReal_min
would require its separate finiteness hypotheses. Do not convert infinity to
zero through a totalized real-valued conversion.

For a=0, tau_Q=0 and the budget is vacuous. Use the explicit global zero
solution, local uniqueness including normalized pressure, and maximality.
No division by initial enstrophy or positive-cutoff premise is needed.
For a!=0, the accepted smooth representative theorem and CUTOFF_POS derive
positive cutoff; they do not add a hypothesis.

For local uniqueness choose one original Q01 Leray-Hopf witness once, compare
both fronts to it, and upgrade cube-a.e. equality at every positive time to
pointwise equality. Pressure equality at t=0 follows by right continuity.
For the root apply R0 once and retain one global pair. Comparison on all
shorter compact restrictions proves agreement on the whole original path.

5. REUSE BOUNDARIES

1. Fixed-N existence is not a cutoff-uniform physical local lifespan.
2. H^(r+2)->H^r smoothness is not same-space Banach ODE local existence.
3. Compactness requires uniform bounds/moduli and does not itself recover nonlinear PDE, pressure, trace or smoothness.
4. A scalar cubic estimate consumes its derivative/cubic premises; it does not prove them for NSE.
5. One physical-path Fourier budget is not AX6 uniform Galerkin budget.
6. Existing global-horizon Sobolev flow/regularity assumptions are not a local construction.
7. Generic Zorn/gluing/ODE time translation is not a physical extension theorem.
8. R0 accepts an actual cap; it does not produce that cap from the WFB budget.
9. Strict cutoff<Tmax is sufficient; do not insert two-sided analytic admission or 2*cutoff<Tmax.
10. Extended nonnegative sums/integrals retain infinity; totalized real integrals are not finiteness predicates.
11. Only b<Tmax is available for closed-slab scalar estimates; do not assume endpoint regularity.
12. Use one fixed LH witness to compare fronts and one fixed global R0 witness for whole-trajectory agreement.

The generic finite-window compactness theorem
CRITICAL_GALERKIN_H0_L2H1_SUBSEQUENCE takes explicit uniform bounds and has
no AX6 premise. It is the relevant construction candidate. By contrast,
AX6_UNIFORM_STRONG_BOUNDS and related packaged global results retain an
explicit AX6Statement premise. The small-critical-data route retains its
smallness hypothesis. Neither silently covers arbitrary smooth data.

The existing arbitrary-field periodic Sobolev, interpolation, convection
Holder, Cauchy-Schwarz and integration-by-parts results are real reusable
ingredients. The displayed Hessian/nonlinear/enstrophy final endpoints are
still finite-synthesis/Galerkin statements; truncating an actual NSE solution
does not make its truncation an actual Galerkin solution.
