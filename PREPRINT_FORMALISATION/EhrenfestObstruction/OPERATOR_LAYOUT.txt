5. E04 DIRECT GENERATOR / RESOLVENT ROUTE (NEW GENERIC PROOFS)

Proposed module UnitaryGenerator.lean:
  Define the generator as a native LinearPMap L_N:
    domain = {x | exists y, HasDerivAt (t -> U_N(t)x) y 0};
    L_N x = the unique derivative y.
  Prove that the domain is a complex submodule and the derivative action is
  complex-linear. Equivalently construct its graph submodule and use the
  native graph-to-LinearPMap API after proving single-valuedness.
  Define H_N = (i*hbar) scalar-multiple L_N.
  Prove on Core_N that L_N(j_N psi)=(-i/hbar)*H0_N(j_N psi);
  hence H0_N <= H_N and the generator domain is dense.

  Prove generator closedness from the integral identity
    U_N(t)x-x = integral_0^t U_N(s)(L_N x) ds,
  passing to limits in (x,L_N x), then differentiating at t=0.
  Prove skew symmetry of L_N by differentiating preservation of inner product.

Proposed module UnitaryResolvent.lean:
  Construct bounded Bochner integral operators
    R_+ x = integral_0^infinity exp(-t)*U_N(t)x dt;
    R_- x = integral_0^infinity exp(-t)*U_N(-t)x dt.
  Prove convergence from ||U_N(t)x||=||x|| and integrability of exp(-t).
  Prove R_+ x and R_- x lie in the generator domain and
    (I-L_N)R_+ x=x; (I+L_N)R_- x=x.
  Deduce surjectivity of I-L_N and I+L_N.
  Prove the generic dense-domain skew-symmetry plus shifted-surjectivity lemma:
    L_N.adjoint = -L_N.
  Deduce IsSelfAdjoint H_N, with adjoint-domain equality explicit.
  ALL of these generator/resolvent implications are proposed new proofs.
  Existing LinearPMap adjoint infrastructure supports their expression but
  does not prove them automatically.

Proposed module TransportCore.lean:
  Prove Core_N is a core of H_N by compactly supported TIME convolution:
    x_epsilon = integral rho_epsilon(t) U_N(t)x dt.
  For x in domain(L_N), x_epsilon -> x and L_N x_epsilon -> L_N x.
  For fixed epsilon approximate x in L2 by j_N psi_j. The smoothed psi_j stay
  in Core_N because the union of transported compact supports over compact
  time support is compact and the integrand is jointly smooth.
  Differentiate convolution by the kernel:
    L_N(int rho(t) U_N(t)z dt) = -int rho'(t) U_N(t)z dt.
  This bounds the derivative error using ||z_j-z||, without assuming bounds
  on derivatives of the unsmoothed approximants.
  Diagonalize the two approximation parameters to obtain graph-norm density.
  Conclude H_N.HasCore D0_N and H0_N.closure = H_N.

  Do not invoke closureHasCore as proof of this identification: it only
  describes the closure already defined, not the independently constructed H_N.
