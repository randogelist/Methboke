3. CONSTRUCTIVE B-TIER DERIVATION

Authoritative cumulative argument:
CLAY_ADMISSION_CANDIDATE_01_B_TIER.txt, Sections 1-8.

3.1 Finite cylinder bounds.
If G_nu(a;epsilon,L) and 0<T<L, continuity of U and grad U on the compact
cylinder gives finite M_T=max|U| and G_T=max||grad U||_op. Volume one gives
  integral_0^T ||U(t)||_6^4 dt <= T*M_T^4,
  integral_0^T ||grad U(t)||_infinity dt <= T*G_T.
Uniform continuity gives U(t)->a uniformly, hence strongly in L2.

3.2 Identification with the original weak trajectory.
For an LH solution u with the same datum, F_RELATIVE_ENERGY gives
  E(t)<=2*integral_0^t g(s)E(s)ds,
  E(t)=||u(t)-U(t)||_2^2, g(t)<=G_T, E(0)=0.
Gronwall yields equality. Weak continuity and the continuous comparison
identify representatives at every time. Arbitrary T<L covers [0,L).

3.3 Finite-cap closure R0.
Zero has the explicit zero solution. Otherwise choose delta=3*tau_Q/2.
F_QFB supplies a smooth tail V,q for the same LH trajectory from delta.
On (delta,T_Q), U and V agree in L2, hence pointwise by spatial continuity.
Their pressure gradients agree by NSE; normalized pressures therefore agree.
Choose eta strictly inside the overlap. Patching before/after eta preserves
all derivatives, NSE and the initial trace. Thus D_Q(nu) subset R_nu.
The same proof works for any analytic cap L>tau_Q. L=tau_Q has no guaranteed
open overlap and is not silently included.

3.4 Remaining universal admission R1.
COVER_nu means D_germ(nu) subset D_Q(nu).
Under the five recorded foundation interfaces, FB_nu iff COVER_nu:
  Forward: identify an admitted analytic germ with the global classical
  solution, use positive-time analyticity and patch the negative germ.
  Reverse: advance any smooth local solution to an interior positive time.
  Its analytic neighborhood supplies a germ. COVER supplies finite-cap
  admission; 3.3 supplies a global solution; local uniqueness patches back
  to the original initial time.
This reduction does not prove either universal assertion.

3.5 Explicit admissible family.
For a finite Fourier Beltrami datum curl a=mu*a, div a=0, mean a=0,
Delta a=-mu^2*a. Set U(t)=exp(-nu*mu^2*t)*a and
p=-|U|^2/2+mean(|U|^2/2). Since U cross curl U=0, the convection term is
exactly cancelled by grad p. This actual solution is jointly analytic for
all real t and has arbitrary amplitude. The ABC/BAMBUS family is a concrete
three-parameter example. This B-tier statement needs no QFB premise.

3.6 Analyticity distinction.
Spatially analytic shear data with coefficients exp(-rho*k) can have
zero time-Taylor radius at t=0 under heat evolution. Thus spatial analytic
input alone does not supply the required jointly space-time analytic germ.
No formal Taylor recursion is counted as a convergence certificate.
