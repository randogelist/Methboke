ROOT CONTRACTS
==============
Fefferman_C_Family_obstruction:
  For every nu>0, every finite N>=1, and every injective a:Fin N -> R3,
  there exist ONE common tuple (U,p,F,K) for that configuration such that:
  F is globally C-infinity and compactly supported in R3 x (0,infinity);
  U,p are C-infinity on R3 x [0,1), solve NSE with viscosity nu and force F,
  div U=0 and U(0)=0;
  one compact K contains all preterminal velocity/pressure supports;
  sup_{0<=t<1} ||U(t)||_2^2 is finite;
  for each j, U is unbounded in every preterminal spacetime neighborhood
  of (a_j,1);
  no global smooth uniformly finite-energy solution exists with the SAME
  viscosity, zero datum and prescribed force F.

Fefferman_D_Family_obstruction:
  The corresponding statement for injective a:Fin N -> T3, with globally
  smooth periodic F compactly supported in time; U,p periodic and smooth
  before time one; zero datum; bounded preterminal cell energy; local blowup
  at every (a_j,1); and no global smooth periodic velocity/PRESSURE pair
  solving the same data and force. The competitor class has no additional
  uniform-in-time energy premise.

The force may depend on N and a: forall configuration, exists F,U,p.
Neither root asserts one fixed force works for every configuration.
The conclusion is at least N prescribed singular locations, not exactly N.
These are finite-configuration existence statements, not injective or
continuous parametrizations modulo scaling, rotations or permutations.

ONE-WAY LITERAL ADAPTERS
========================
Fefferman_C_Family_obstruction -> classical Fefferman C:
  choose N=1 and center0; initial datum0 is Schwartz and divergence-free.
Fefferman_D_Family_obstruction -> classical Fefferman D:
  choose N=1 and torus center0; datum0 is smooth periodic.
Compact force support gives all required derivative/decay bounds.
Pressure periodicity is retained for D, including the official erratum.
No reverse implication from bare C/D to the stronger compact-seed family
structure is asserted.

PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL DEPENDENCY
===============================
Published compact/rest/blowup seed
  -> compact local growth point + recentering
  -> delayed compressed/translated copies
  -> finite separated placement
  -> smooth disjoint assembly + exact PDE + admitted force
  -> bounded energy + growth at each prescribed point
  -> [generic whole-space comparison -> C family]
     [physical-space packing + periodization + periodic comparison -> D family].

The literature seed is a substantive imported mathematical theorem.
The family closure is our B-tier corollary of that theorem, not a reconstruction
of the seed proof and not a new singularity mechanism.

SEMANTIC EXCLUSIONS
===================
1. At the inspected Mathlib pins, ContDiff infinity is ordinary C-infinity.
   ContDiff omega/top means analytic. Methlib's concrete C/D smooth predicates
   use top, so their existing *_BREAKDOWN_IFF_* endpoints cannot admit the
   nonzero compactly supported smooth-force seed unchanged.
2. Methboke's unforced InitialClassicalFront uniqueness and unforced energy
   identity do not apply to a nonzero prescribed force. A common-force
   difference equation cancels forcing only after both equations are supplied.
3. Pure Gaussian COUNTER_EXAMPLE_FAMILY_B still fails smooth-force admission.
   The present roots use the independent published smooth-force seed.
4. Family coordinates are diagnostics, not a replacement for the full field.
   Global force orthogonality cannot hold throughout a nontrivial flow from rest.
5. Arbitrary time, rotations, overlapping sums, infinite sums, exact singular-set
   cardinality and quotient-cardinality novelty are outside the named roots.
