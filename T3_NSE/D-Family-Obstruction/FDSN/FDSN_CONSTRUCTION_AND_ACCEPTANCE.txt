Date: 20 November 2026. Canonical statement: Section 26, FDSN v1.0.

29.1 The one root

  FEFFERMAN_D_SPECTRAL_NONRIGIDITY:
    forall nu,T,U,p,F, Seed_(nu,T)(U,p,F) -> Family_(nu,T)(U,p,F).

The native namespace is MethbokeAddon.FDSN. The spatial torus is represented
by genuinely unit-periodic real fields on Vec, with Fourier frequencies
Fin 3 -> Z. The viscosity, terminal time, initial datum, and spatial mean
are unrestricted subject to the original Seed hypotheses. No polynomial
terminal derivative-growth condition, desired family, or fixed-force
nonuniqueness premise has been added to Seed.

The formal Family uses one interval (a,b), one tstar, one epsilon, and the
same U_lambda and F_lambda in all five clauses. The helical coordinate is
the literal Hermitian coordinate in an explicitly specified orthonormal
curl-eigenframe. A stronger construction proves the conclusion for every
array of complex-linear coordinate observations; genuine helical frames
are then an explicit specialization. The mean is preserved separately.

29.2 Active triads from actual forced evolution

The native proof uses modewise estimates instead of differentiating an
infinite weighted energy sum. Write c_k(t)=Fourier(U(t))(k). Suppose no
active noncollinear triad exists throughout a final interval. On every
active output k, every nonzero-input convection term either has a vanishing
input, or has collinear frequencies and vanishes by incompressibility.
The remaining term is the exact mean-advection term

  N_k = 2*pi*i*(k . c_0)*c_k.

Reality makes c_0 real, so this term has zero real modal energy transfer.
For an inactive output, the real energy transfer vanishes because c_k=0.
The actual Fourier-projected forced NSE consequently gives, componentwise,

  d/dt |c_(k,j)|^2 <= |c_(k,j)|^2 + |P_k Fhat_(k,j)|^2.

Smooth periodic forcing on the compact slab [0,T] has a common fourth-order
Fourier envelope. A single strictly preterminal smooth slice has the same
kind of envelope. Native finite-dimensional Leray contraction controls the
projected force, including its zero mode. Scalar Gronwall propagates a
uniform summable envelope through the final interval. Actual Fourier
reconstruction then bounds the physical velocity uniformly, contradicting
the cofinal sup-velocity blowup in Seed.

Thus every (s,T), s in [0,T), contains a time tstar and nonzero p,q,p+q
with all three vector coefficients nonzero and p,q noncollinear. This is
derived from the original Seed, rather than supplied as a new hypothesis.

29.3 One concrete localized pair

Choose k=p+q and a smooth real beta with beta(tstar)=1 and compact support
inside one interval (a,b) compactly contained in (s,T). The same beta and
the same selected k serve all parameter values. Set epsilon=pi/4 and

  h_lambda(t,x) = 2 Re [ exp(2*pi*i*k.x)
                        (exp(i*lambda*beta(t))-1) c_k(t) ],
  U_lambda = U + h_lambda,

  F_lambda = F + partial_t h_lambda - nu*Delta h_lambda
                 + (U.grad)h_lambda + (h_lambda.grad)U
                 + (h_lambda.grad)h_lambda.

The construction rotates exactly the conjugate pair +/-k. It is real,
periodic, and divergence-free. Actual Fourier reconstruction identifies
the finite correction with the pair multiplier. Every complex-linear
observation of each affected vector coefficient is multiplied by a unit
complex number; all other modes are unchanged. Hence every helical modal
power and the complete zero mode are exactly preserved.

The coefficient c_k is only required to be smooth on the preterminal open
interval. Compact interior support of beta extends the correction smoothly
by zero in time. The proof derives global joint smoothness in parameter,
time and space; no extension of the blowing-up seed through T is assumed.

Native differential identities prove that the displayed residual force
gives the actual forced NSE with the original pressure. Locality proves
that the force correction has no larger closed temporal support than h.
Thus F_lambda remains globally smooth, periodic, and compactly supported
in positive time. Initial data and exact terminal velocity/force germs
agree with the seed, and the same terminal blowup is retained.

29.4 Translation separation at the same time

Noncollinearity implies that p and q are different from both k and -k.
Choose nonzero Cartesian coordinates of the three active vector modes.
Their resonant correlation is a nonzero scalar product. Under translation
by y the three Fourier characters cancel because p+q=k. Rotating only k
multiplies this correlation by a parameter phase. At beta(tstar)=1,
distinct lambda,eta with |lambda|,|eta|<pi/4 have distinct phases. Therefore
the full spatial fields U_lambda(tstar) and U_eta(tstar) cannot be translates.
Cartesian coordinates are used only for this certificate; helical power
preservation is established independently for all complex-linear modal
observations and hence for the prescribed helical coordinates.

29.5 Actual projected-force separation

For two members let h=U_eta-U_lambda. Its Fourier support is contained in
the same pair +/-k. At output k the exact nonlinear difference reduces to
mean advection; all other mixed terms vanish by incompressibility and
frequency collinearity. If the corresponding projected force component
agreed throughout the interval before tstar, the selected difference z
would satisfy the actual scalar equation

  z' = -(nu*4*pi^2*|k|^2 + 2*pi*i*(k.mean(U))) z.

Its norm square is nonincreasing since nu>0. At a positive time before
the bump it is zero, so it remains zero through tstar. This contradicts
the distinct selected Fourier coefficients at tstar. Consequently the
projected force histories differ at an interior time. The projector used
here is the native coefficientLeray applied to actual force Fourier
coefficients, with P_0 the identity. No pressure-recovery assumption or
unforced uniqueness theorem is substituted for the forced comparison.

29.6 Every fixed smooth order

For a jointly smooth correction G(lambda,z) with G(0,z)=0, each actual
iterated Frechet derivative in z remains jointly smooth. On a compact
spacetime slab and a compact parameter interval, its parameter derivative
is bounded. The mean-value inequality gives a bound C_n*|lambda| for
the n-th derivative. A finite sum supplies one constant for all n<=r.
Applying this argument to both actual corrections proves FDSN.5 in the
literal C^r convention: the sum of the suprema of full time-space Frechet
derivatives through order r over the compact unit-cell slab. The constant
may depend on r, the seed, and the chosen interval.

29.7 Physical Leray presentation

The native physicalLerayProjection is the actual smooth Fourier synthesis
of coefficientLeray applied to the Fourier coefficients of a smooth
periodic vector field. Its companion lemmas prove smoothness, periodicity,
divergence freedom, exact Fourier recovery, and preservation of the zero
mode. The Helmholtz identity identifies it as v + grad(pi) for a smooth
periodic scalar pi, with the sign fixed by this definition.

The presentation theorem retains the same interval, comparison time,
parameter radius, velocities, forces, and all five original clauses. It
also states that for each pair of distinct admitted parameters there are
a preterminal time t and a point x where

  physicalLerayProjection((F_lambda - F_eta)(t))(x) != 0.

This follows from the unequal actual projected Fourier coefficient: an
identically zero physical field would have every Fourier coefficient zero.
It introduces no additional seed hypothesis or seed-existence claim.
