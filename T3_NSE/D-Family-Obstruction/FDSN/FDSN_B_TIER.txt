25.1 Why this is the selected minimal target

The existing Section 24.3 already gives a compact-time spectral family without
(PG). The present target sharpens its nontriviality: distinct members have a
different translation-invariant third-order Fourier correlation, while every
individual helical modal power remains fixed. One scalar parameter suffices.

The nearest 2026 result is Cao--Chi--Nie, arXiv:2609.10262v4,
Proposition 3.15:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2609.10262v4
It supplies compact spacetime perturbations of the whole-space seed and
absorbs their equation error into the force. The periodic adaptation uses
the same identity. It already includes infinite-dimensional families and
smooth non-isolation. The present target adds a joint spectral constraint
and an explicit nontranslation certificate. It does not retain localization
inside a prescribed spatial ball: a Fourier-pair perturbation is global on
the torus.

Spectral phase preservation is itself older. Yoshimatsu, Okamoto, Schneider,
Kaneda and Farge, Physical Review E 79, 026303 (2009), Section II, randomize
helical coefficient phases while preserving energy and helicity spectra:
  https://www.i2m.univ-amu.fr/perso/kai.schneider/PDF-FILES/yoskf_pre2009_final.pdf
  DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevE.79.026303
Wang, Fang, Wang and Xu, Physical Review Fluids 9, 114603 (2024), describe
Fourier-phase control through designed forcing:
  https://journals.aps.org/prfluids/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevFluids.9.114603

Thus the candidate literature contribution is the exact joint realization
inside a classical blowup family with fixed initial and terminal histories.
Neither phase ambiguity nor the residual-compensation mechanism is claimed
new. Liu's 2026 observation-fiber framework remains relevant as already
recorded in Section 23. Global priority and publication significance have
not been established.

25.2 Exact minimal theorem statement

Assume (S1)--(S3) of Section 24.1. Thus nu>0, 0<T<infinity, (U,p,F) is a real
smooth periodic forced NSE solution for 0<=t<T, the force is globally smooth
and admissible, the initial datum a is fixed, energy and dissipation are
finite, and limsup_(t up T)||U(t)||_infinity=infinity.

No hypothesis (PG) is imposed.

For every prescribed s with 0<=s<T, there exist an open interval
I=(a_I,b_I) with s<a_I<b_I<T, a time t_* in I, epsilon>0, and a smooth
one-parameter family (U_lambda,p,F_lambda), |lambda|<epsilon, such that:

(N1) The family solves the same periodic NSE at viscosity nu; lambda=0
     is the original seed. For t outside I,
         U_lambda(t)=U(t)  (where t<T),
         F_lambda(t)=F(t)  (for all globally defined times).
     Consequently the initial datum, exact late velocity history, and
     exact late force history agree. Each member blows up at the same T.

(N2) Fix orthonormal helical frames once and write c_sigma(k,t) for the
     velocity coefficients, sigma in {+,-}. For all k!=0 and all t<T,
         |c_(lambda,sigma)(k,t)|^2=|c_sigma(k,t)|^2,
     and the mean is unchanged.

(N3) At the single fixed time t_*, distinct parameters are not related
     by any spatial translation:
         for lambda!=eta and every y in T^3,
         U_lambda(t_*, .) != U_eta(t_*, .+y).
     No claim of inequivalence under all possible rotations or symmetries
     is included.

(N4) Distinct parameters have different effective force histories:
         Leray(F_lambda) != Leray(F_eta) on T^3 x [0,T).
     Equivalently, their full force difference is not a periodic
     pressure-gradient change.

(N5) For every fixed nonnegative integer r,
         ||U_lambda-U||_(C^r(T^3 x closure(I)))
          + ||F_lambda-F||_(C^r(T^3 x closure(I)))
             <= C_r |lambda|
     for sufficiently small lambda. The constant may depend on r,
     the seed, the chosen interval, and the chosen phase bump.
     This gives arbitrary closeness for every prescribed finite
     collection of smooth seminorms.

Here every member has its own force. The fixed-force uniqueness theorem
is respected.

25.3 Active-triad lemma: no additional seed obligation

For every s<T there is some t_* in (s,T) and a noncollinear triad
         k_1+k_2=k_3
with all three Fourier coefficients of U nonzero at t_*.

Proof. Write A_n(t)=Fourier(U)(n,t). The Fourier convection term is
         Fourier((U.grad)U)(n)
            = 2 pi i sum_(p+q=n) (A_p . q) A_q.

Suppose no active noncollinear triad occurs at any time in (s,T).
In a real modal energy-transfer summand, all of A_p,A_q,A_n must be
nonzero to contribute. When p,q are nonzero and noncollinear, that
would be a forbidden triad. When p,q are collinear,
A_p.q=0 by p.A_p=0. When q=0 the derivative factor is zero. When
p=0 the summand paired with conjugate(A_n) is
         2 pi i (A_0.n) |A_n|^2,
which is purely imaginary since A_0 is real. The zero output mode
also has zero convection contribution.

Therefore every real nonlinear modal energy-transfer term vanishes.
The pressure term is orthogonal to the divergence-free coefficients.
Summing with the H^2 weights yields
         (1/2) d_t ||U||_(H^2)^2
           + nu ||grad U||_(H^2)^2
             = Re <F,U>_(H^2).
By the norm inequality (or its regularized zero-norm version),
         ||U(t)||_(H^2)
            <= ||U(s)||_(H^2) + integral_s^t ||F(r)||_(H^2) dr.
The right side stays finite up to T, since F is smooth through T.
The embedding H^2(T^3) -> L-infinity(T^3) contradicts the assumed blowup.

Every Fourier sum and differentiation above is justified on a compact
preterminal slab by smoothness. The argument allows the Fourier support
to vary with time; no persistent-support assumption was made. QED.

Choose helicities sigma_j for which c_(sigma_j)(k_j,t_*)!=0.
Continuity supplies I compactly contained in (s,T), containing t_*,
on which their product
         B_U(t)=c_(sigma_1)(k_1,t)c_(sigma_2)(k_2,t)
                    conjugate(c_(sigma_3)(k_3,t))
never vanishes. Noncollinearity ensures that the three mode pairs
{+/- k_j} are distinct. This triad is obtained from the seed hypotheses,
not assumed as an unverified extra property of the published seed.

25.4 Explicit one-pair construction

Choose a real bump beta in C_c^infinity(I) with
         0<=beta<=1,   beta(t_*)=1.
Take epsilon=pi/4. Rotate only the conjugate pair +/- k_3:
         Fourier(U_lambda)(k_3,t)
             = exp(i lambda beta(t)) Fourier(U)(k_3,t),
         Fourier(U_lambda)(-k_3,t)
             = exp(-i lambda beta(t)) Fourier(U)(-k_3,t).
Every other Fourier coefficient, including the mean, is left unchanged.

Reality and incompressibility follow immediately. The scalar multiplier
acts on both helicities equally, so (N2) holds for every individual
helical modal power. The correction
         h_lambda=U_lambda-U
has only the two spatial Fourier frequencies +/- k_3 and has smooth
time support compactly inside I.

Keep p_lambda=p and define for t<T
         F_lambda=F + partial_t h_lambda - nu Delta h_lambda
                    + (U.grad)h_lambda + (h_lambda.grad)U
                    + (h_lambda.grad)h_lambda.              (MN1)
Define F_lambda=F outside the correction time support, including t>=T.

Expanding the momentum equation proves the NSE exactly. Every correction
in (MN1) vanishes outside the fixed compact regular time slab and is
smooth there to all orders. The zero extension is therefore globally
smooth. All globally stipulated temporal support/decay conditions of the
seed force are retained. No estimate as t approaches T is needed.

The exact late velocity agreement preserves the original unbounded-speed
limsup. Classical uniqueness on every shorter time interval rules out
a global smooth competitor with the same initial datum and F_lambda.

25.5 Explicit nontranslation and force-gauge certificates

Under a translation x -> x+y, every helical coefficient at k gains the
phase exp(2 pi i k.y). The phases cancel in B_U because k_1+k_2=k_3.
Thus B_U is invariant under spatial translations, in the fixed helical
frame convention.

In the constructed family,
         B_(U_lambda)(t)=exp(-i lambda beta(t)) B_U(t).
At t=t_* this is
         B_(U_lambda)(t_*)=exp(-i lambda) B_U(t_*).
The base product is nonzero and |lambda-eta|<pi/2. Distinct parameters
give distinct products, proving (N3). In particular, even a time-dependent
spatial translation cannot identify the complete trajectories.

If Leray(F_lambda)=Leray(F_eta) throughout the preterminal interval, then
the two velocities solve the same projected NSE with the same initial
datum. The classical difference-energy estimate and Gronwall, on every
compact subinterval, imply U_lambda=U_eta. This contradicts (N3), so (N4)
holds. The Leray projector retains the zero mode; equality of projections
is precisely equality modulo gradients of globally defined periodic
scalar pressures.

25.6 Exact budgets, smooth smallness, and optional infinite family

For every real q and every t<T, (N2) and the unchanged mean imply
         ||U_lambda(t)||_(H^q)=||U(t)||_(H^q).
Thus kinetic energy, enstrophy, helicity, dissipation history and
         Q_(U_lambda)(t)=||grad U_lambda(t)||_2^2/(4 pi^2)=Q_U(t)
agree exactly. Their time integrals agree, including as extended
nonnegative integrals where necessary.

The instantaneous total force work also agrees:
         integral_(T^3) F_lambda.U_lambda
             = (1/2) d_t ||U_lambda||_2^2
                    + nu ||grad U_lambda||_2^2
             = integral_(T^3) F.U.                          (MN2)
This follows from the classical energy identity for each smooth slice.
No unforced continuation theorem is being applied to these forced flows.

For each fixed r, time differentiation of
exp(i lambda beta)-1 and the finite Fourier-pair expression gives
||h_lambda||_(C^(r+2)) <= C'_r |lambda| on the compact slab.
The coefficients of U and their required derivatives are bounded there.
Formula (MN1) yields (N5). No uniform constant across all derivative
orders is asserted.

An infinite-dimensional version is immediate but is not needed for the
minimal target: replace lambda beta by theta in the fixed-support space
         E_K={theta in C^infinity(R;R): supp theta subset K},
where K is a compact interval with nonempty interior and K subset I,
and restrict ||theta||_infinity<pi/4. Distinct theta,eta differ at some
time in K, where B_U!=0, so the same bispectral argument distinguishes
their translation classes. The time distinguishing a pair may depend
on that pair. Every neighborhood defined by finitely many smooth
seminorms contains the image of a sufficiently small neighborhood of
zero in E_K. No open subset of the entire force space is asserted.

26.1 Frozen source predicate: Seed_(nu,T)(U,p,F)

The predicate Seed contains all of the following:

(H1) nu>0, 0<T<infinity, and the spatial domain is the unit three-torus.
     U and p are real and smooth on T^3 x [0,T), with periodic pressure.

(H2) F belongs to C_c^infinity(T^3 x (0,infinity);R^3), and
       partial_t U - nu Delta U + (U.grad)U + grad p = F,
       div U=0.
     The initial datum a=U(0) is smooth and divergence free.
     No zero-mean assumption on a is required.

(H3) The seed has bounded preterminal kinetic energy and finite integrated
     dissipation:
       sup_(0<=t<T) ||U(t)||_2^2
         + integral_0^T ||grad U(t)||_2^2 dt < infinity.

(H4) The seed has velocity blowup at T:
       limsup_(t up T) ||U(t)||_infinity = infinity.

The seed predicate does not contain (PG), a phase family, a fixed-force
nonuniqueness assumption, or the desired spectral-nonrigidity conclusion.
The admissible source seed is the external input to the theorem.
The full proof of the cited source's existence theorem is not re-audited
merely by assembling this conditional implication.

For definiteness, the canonical v1.0 statement uses compact temporal
support of F. The already-discussed broader temporal-decay variant can
be cited separately; it is not silently substituted for (H2).

26.2 Frozen conclusion predicate: Family_(nu,T)(U,p,F)

Fix orthonormal helical frames h_sigma(k), sigma in {+1,-1}, k!=0, with
       2 pi i k cross h_sigma(k)=sigma 2 pi |k| h_sigma(k),
and compatible conjugacy between k and -k. Write c_sigma(k,t) for the
helical coefficients of U and c_(lambda,sigma)(k,t) for those of U_lambda.
Let Leray denote the periodic Leray projector retaining its zero mode.

Family means:
  For every s in [0,T), there exist one open interval I compactly
  contained in (s,T), one t_* in I, one epsilon>0, and one smooth family
       (U_lambda,p,F_lambda),   |lambda|<epsilon,
  such that (U_0,F_0)=(U,F), and FDSN.1--FDSN.5 all hold for this SAME
  interval, comparison time, and family.

FDSN.1 -- EXACT_REALIZATION_AND_AGREEMENT

  Each (U_lambda,p,F_lambda) solves the periodic NSE at viscosity nu,
  with U_lambda(0)=U(0) and globally admissible
       F_lambda in C_c^infinity(T^3 x (0,infinity);R^3).
  The difference U_lambda-U extends by zero to a member of
       C_c^infinity(T^3 x I;R^3),
  and F_lambda-F belongs to the same space.
  Thus the pressure is unchanged, the initial and exact late velocity
  and force histories agree, and each member blows up at the same T.

FDSN.2 -- COMPLETE_HELICAL_POWER_PRESERVATION

  For all |lambda|<epsilon, all k!=0, all sigma, and all 0<=t<T,
       |c_(lambda,sigma)(k,t)|^2=|c_sigma(k,t)|^2,
       Fourier(U_lambda)(0,t)=Fourier(U)(0,t).

FDSN.3 -- TRANSLATION_SEPARATION

  For all lambda!=eta in the parameter interval and every y in T^3,
       U_lambda(t_*,.) is not identical to U_eta(t_*,.+y).
  This is a distinction between entire spatial fields at the common
  time t_*, not an assertion of pointwise inequality at every x.

FDSN.4 -- PROJECTED_FORCE_SEPARATION

  For all distinct lambda,eta,
       Leray(F_lambda-F_eta) is not identically zero
       on T^3 x [0,T).
  This is separation of the complete force histories, not necessarily
  inequality at every time or point.

FDSN.5 -- SMOOTH_SMALLNESS

  For every integer r>=0 there exist finite C_r and delta_r>0 such that,
  for |lambda|<min(epsilon,delta_r),
       ||U_lambda-U||_(C^r(T^3 x closure(I)))
        + ||F_lambda-F||_(C^r(T^3 x closure(I)))
           <= C_r |lambda|.
  There is no claim of a constant uniform over all derivative orders.

The quantifier order is part of the theorem:
  forall seed, forall s, exists I,t_*,epsilon,family,
  followed by the universal parameter/mode/time requirements.
It must not be weakened to separate families satisfying separate clauses.

26.3 The one main theorem

FEFFERMAN_D_SPECTRAL_NONRIGIDITY (FDSN):

  For every nu,T,U,p,F,
       Seed_(nu,T)(U,p,F) ==> Family_(nu,T)(U,p,F).

This is the single root endpoint for the current spectral-nonrigidity
chain. The following statements are supporting lemmas for that endpoint,
not separately adopted project goals.

26.4 Supporting lemma register

FDSN.L1 -- ACTIVE_TRIAD
  Input: Seed_(nu,T)(U,p,F) and any s in [0,T).
  Output: a time t_* in (s,T) and noncollinear k_1,k_2 with
          k_1+k_2=k_3 and all three Fourier coefficients nonzero.
  Proof location: Section 25.3.
  Argument: absence of an active noncollinear triad throughout (s,T)
  makes every real nonlinear modal energy transfer zero. The resulting
  forced linear H^2 bound contradicts the L-infinity blowup.

FDSN.L2 -- LOCAL_PHASE_FAMILY
  Inputs: L1 and seed smoothness.
  Output: an interval I, nonvanishing helical triad product on I, a
  compactly supported bump beta with beta(t_*)=1, and the family formed
  by rotating only +/- k_3 by exp(+/- i lambda beta(t)).
  The mean and every individual helical modal power are preserved.
  The correction h_lambda has compact time support in I.
  Supplies: FDSN.2 and the velocity part of FDSN.1.
  Proof locations: Sections 25.3--25.4.

FDSN.L3 -- RESIDUAL_REALIZATION
  Inputs: L2, the seed equation, and the compact regular time slab.
  Define:
       F_lambda=F + partial_t h_lambda - nu Delta h_lambda
                  + (U.grad)h_lambda + (h_lambda.grad)U
                  + (h_lambda.grad)h_lambda.
  Output: exact NSE realization, unchanged pressure, smooth admissible
  forcing, common initial datum, and exact late agreement/blowup.
  Supplies: FDSN.1.
  Proof location: Section 25.4.

FDSN.L4 -- BISPECTRAL_SEPARATION
  Input: the family and nonzero triad product from L2.
  Define:
       B_U(t)=c_(sigma_1)(k_1,t)c_(sigma_2)(k_2,t)
                    conjugate(c_(sigma_3)(k_3,t)).
  Translation phases cancel because k_1+k_2=k_3, but
       B_(U_lambda)(t_*)=exp(-i lambda) B_U(t_*).
  Choosing epsilon<=pi/4 gives pairwise distinct translation classes.
  Supplies: FDSN.3.
  Proof location: Section 25.5.

FDSN.L5 -- PROJECTED_FORCE_SEPARATION
  Inputs: L3, L4, and classical uniqueness on compact preterminal slabs.
  If two projected force histories agreed, the common initial datum
  would give identical velocities, contradicting L4.
  Supplies: FDSN.4.
  Proof location: Section 25.5.

FDSN.L6 -- SMOOTH_PARAMETER_ESTIMATE
  Inputs: the finite Fourier-pair construction in L2 and residual in L3.
  Differentiate exp(i lambda beta)-1 at each fixed order on the compact
  slab, and estimate the residual using bounded seed derivatives there.
  Supplies: FDSN.5.
  Proof location: Section 25.6.

28.6 Most attainable formal construction order

First, prepare PDE-independent leaves in the chosen existing compiler pin:
  (i) unit norm and conjugacy of exp(i theta);
  (ii) injectivity for the small phase interval;
  (iii) norm(exp(i theta)-1)<=abs(theta);
  (iv) normalized smooth bump with its exact support;
  (v) local persistence of a nonzero continuous triad product.

Next, implement the actual two-mode correction directly, rather than
starting with a general infinite Fourier phase operator. Componentwise,
for k_*=k_3 and z_lambda(t)=exp(i lambda beta(t)),
  h_lambda(t,x)
    =(z_lambda(t)-1) Fourier(U)(k_*,t) exp(2 pi i k_*.x)
      + conjugate((z_lambda(t)-1) Fourier(U)(k_*,t) exp(2 pi i k_*.x)).
The equality with a real vector field and its actual Fourier coefficients
must be proved using the selected native definitions. This finite formula
makes compact-time smoothness and all fixed-order estimates direct.

Then:
  - construct helical coefficient functionals and prove their linearity;
  - use the exact convection expansion for residual realization;
  - prove the translation character/bispectrum algebra;
  - finish the genuinely analytic active-triad and projected-force bridges;
  - derive physical quadratic observations and total force work;
  - assemble the unchanged FDSN v1.0 root with shared witnesses.

The helical frame is discrete in k; no frequency-continuous frame is
required. Scalar phase preservation can first be proved for arbitrary
complex-linear coordinate functionals, then specialized to a correctly
constructed helical frame.

For K13 either:
  Route P: use periodic pressure recovery plus its time-smoothness adapter,
           then the same-force classical uniqueness theorem; or
  Route E: prove the projected-force difference-energy inequality and
           apply the existing generic Gronwall endpoint.
Both routes require real new adapters. The generic comparison theorem
cannot be used by merely assuming its PDE energy hypothesis as the
desired physical conclusion.
