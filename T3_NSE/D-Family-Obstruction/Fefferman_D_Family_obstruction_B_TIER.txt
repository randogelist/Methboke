DEFINITIONS
============
Let Torus3=R^3/Z^3 with its flat quotient metric d_T and unit-volume measure.
Velocity, pressure, and force may equivalently be represented by unit-periodic
lifts on R^3. Time is written first: u(t,x), p(t,x), f(t,x).

NSE_nu(u,p,f) means
  partial_t u+(u dot grad)u-nu*Delta u+grad p=f,   div u=0,
with nu>0. Pressure is scalar. The viscosity multiplies only the Laplacian.
Initial datum is u(0,x)=0.

For a unit-periodic lift U define
  E_T(U,t)=integral_{[0,1]^3} |U(t,x)|^2 dx.
The value is independent of the fundamental cube. Integrability is part of
the energy assertion; no totalized integral is used to conceal divergence.

For c in Torus3, define LocalBlowup(U,c,1) by:
  for every M>0, epsilon>0 and delta>0, there exist 0<t<1 and x in Torus3
  such that 1-t<delta, d_T(x,c)<epsilon, and |U(t,x)|>M.
This is local unboundedness in every left spacetime neighborhood of (c,1).
It does not assert that c is the only singular point.

AdmissiblePeriodicForce(F) means:
  F is C-infinity on Torus3 times R;
  for some 0<a<b<infinity, F(t,x)=0 whenever t is outside [a,b].
Equivalently its periodic lift is globally smooth and compactly supported in
time. A nonzero periodic lift is NOT compactly supported in space on R^3.
All mixed derivatives therefore satisfy, for each multi-index alpha, m>=0
and k>=0,
  |partial_x^alpha partial_t^m F(t,x)|
    <= C_(alpha,m,k)*(1+t)^(-k),  t>=0,
with finite constants allowed to depend on the constructed finite family.

A GlobalPeriodicCompetitor(nu,F) consists of velocity V and pressure Q,
both smooth on Torus3 times [0,infinity), both periodic as lifts, satisfying
NSE_nu(V,Q,F) and V(0)=0. No global-in-time energy bound, spatial support
condition, energy inequality, pressure normalization, or derivative-growth
assumption is added to this competitor definition.

THE EXACT ROOT STATEMENT
========================
Fefferman_D_Family_obstruction :=
  for every nu>0, every integer N>=1, and every injective map
    c : Fin N -> Torus3,
  there exist a prescribed force F and preterminal fields U,P_press such that:

  (D.a) AdmissiblePeriodicForce(F).
  (D.b) U,P_press are jointly C-infinity on Torus3 times [0,1).
  (D.c) The lifts of U,P_press,F are all unit-periodic in space.
  (D.d) NSE_nu(U,P_press,F) holds for 0<=t<1, and U(0)=0.
  (D.e) Every preterminal slice is square-integrable, and
        sup_{0<=t<1} E_T(U,t)<infinity.
  (D.f) For every j in Fin N, LocalBlowup(U,c(j),1).
  (D.g) There is no GlobalPeriodicCompetitor(nu,F).

The theorem promises AT LEAST N prescribed simultaneous singular locations.
It makes no claim of exactly N singular points, an injective parameterization
modulo symmetries, countable-copy closure, or a common force for all families.

COMPLETE B-TIER DERIVATION
==========================

F00. Classical semantics.
-------------------------
Use ordinary C-infinity regularity, meaning every finite derivative order.
In the inspected source this is ContDiff/ContDiffOn at scoped infinity,
with "open scoped ContDiff". Do not silently substitute analytic regularity
or an unrelated wrapper using a different top element.

Periodic pressure is explicitly required. The competitor class is precisely
the smooth periodic class described above. On each compact time interval a
smooth periodic competitor has finite energy automatically, but no uniform
bound as t tends to infinity is imposed.

F01. A common compact carrier and early rest.
---------------------------------------------
Take the seed tuple. Let
  K_star = K union pr_x(tsupport f).
The projection of compact spacetime support is compact, so K_star is compact.
It contains every preterminal spatial support of u and p and every spatial
slice of f at ALL times. This is the carrier used for separation below.

The source gives u=p=0 on an initial time interval. The PDE implies f=0 on
its positive-time interior because all derivatives of u and p vanish there.
Globally f already vanishes at nonpositive times by its positive-time support.
Extend u and p by zero to t<0. Early rest makes the extension smooth across
zero. This operation makes no claim about extending u or p across t=1.

F02. Local growth extraction and recentering.
--------------------------------------------
For each integer n>=1 use SpeedUnboundedAtOne with size n and remaining
time less than 1/n to obtain (t_n,x_n) such that
  0<t_n<1, 1-t_n<1/n, |u(t_n,x_n)|>n.
The nonzero velocity implies x_n in K. Compactness provides a subsequence
x_(n_k) converging to a_star in K, with n_k tending to infinity. Consequently
t_(n_k) tends to one and |u(t_(n_k),x_(n_k))| tends to infinity.
This proves local unboundedness at (a_star,1).

Translate the seed by setting
  u_c(t,y)=u(t,y+a_star),
  p_c(t,y)=p(t,y+a_star), f_c(t,y)=f(t,y+a_star).
Translations preserve every term of NSE, Lebesgue energy, smoothness and rest.
Its common carrier is K_c=K_star-a_star, and its singular accumulation point
is now zero. The extraction/recentering is a new family adapter; it is not
already part of the exported SpeedUnboundedAtOne interface.

Physical viscosity, BEFORE periodic identification.
----------------------------------------------------
For the requested nu>0 define on the whole space
  u_nu(t,y)=sqrt(nu)*u_c(t,y/sqrt(nu)),
  p_nu(t,y)=nu*p_c(t,y/sqrt(nu)),
  f_nu(t,y)=sqrt(nu)*f_c(t,y/sqrt(nu)).
Direct chain rules, or the source's candidate_at_viscosity, give NSE_nu.
The common carrier is K_nu=sqrt(nu)*K_c. Early rest and local blowup at zero
are preserved. The squared L2 energy is multiplied by nu^(5/2).

All viscosity lifting takes place on R^3. Applying x -> x/sqrt(nu) to an
already unit-periodic field would generally change its period and is not
a valid fixed-unit-torus step.

F03. Delayed copies.
--------------------
For each future copy choose lambda_j>1 and a physical center a_j. Define
  t_j=1-lambda_j^(-2),
  s_j(t)=lambda_j^2*(t-1)+1,
  y_j(x)=lambda_j*(x-a_j),
  u_j(t,x)=lambda_j*u_nu(s_j(t),y_j(x)),
  p_j(t,x)=lambda_j^2*p_nu(s_j(t),y_j(x)),
  f_j(t,x)=lambda_j^3*f_nu(s_j(t),y_j(x)).
Use the negative-time zero extension for u_nu,p_nu; use the given globally
smooth f_nu for the force. The velocity and pressure are needed only for t<1,
where s_j(t)<1. The force is defined for every t.

Temporal derivative, advection, viscous Laplacian, and pressure gradient all
acquire the factor lambda_j^3, with the viscosity unchanged. Divergence
acquires factor lambda_j^2. Hence each copy solves the same NSE_nu and is
solenoidal. Its carrier is K_j=a_j+lambda_j^(-1)*K_nu.

The initial datum is zero because s_j(0)=1-lambda_j^2<0. Smoothness across
t=t_j follows from the seed's open interval of rest. The strict inequality
lambda_j>1 matches compressedCandidate's existing formal interface.

F04. Finite placement on the torus.
-----------------------------------
Choose b in R^3 such that no coordinate face of
  Q_b=b+(-1/2,1/2)^3
passes through any of the finitely many requested torus sites. For each
coordinate there are only finitely many forbidden cut values modulo one,
so such a choice exists. Each site c(j) has a unique representative a_j
in Q_b. The representatives are distinct.

Their distances to the boundary of Q_b have a positive minimum. Their
pairwise torus distances also have a positive minimum when N>1; for N=1
only the boundary margin is needed. Choose a positive epsilon smaller than
one fourth of both applicable minima. The epsilon-balls around the a_j are
contained in Q_b and pairwise disjoint, including modulo lattice translates.

The compact K_nu lies in a finite ball about zero. By choosing each lambda_j
sufficiently large, arrange K_j inside the epsilon-ball around a_j.
All velocity, pressure, and ALL-TIME force supports are then separated.
In centered coordinates z=x-b their finite union lies in [-r,r]^3 for
some 0<r<1/2. Such an r exists by the positive boundary margin.
No quarter-cube assumption is imposed on the prescribed sites.

F05. Smooth finite sums and local germs.
----------------------------------------
Set
  U_R=sum_j u_j, P_R=sum_j p_j, F_R=sum_j f_j.
There are only finitely many terms. Smoothness is preserved on the relevant
time domains. The union K_R=union_j K_j is compact.

At any spatial point at most one K_j is active. The finitely many other
closed carriers have a neighborhood on which their fields vanish for every
time. Thus near every preterminal spacetime point the summed triple equals
one copy, or equals zero. This is a local equality of germs, strong enough
to transfer time and spatial derivatives of every required order.

F06. Exact PDE, including the nonlinear term.
---------------------------------------------
Locality now gives
  div U_R=0,
  partial_t U_R+(U_R dot grad)U_R-nu*Delta U_R+grad P_R=F_R.
Equivalently, every cross term (u_i dot grad)u_j for i!=j vanishes because
the derivative of a smooth supported function has support in the same
closed carrier. Hence advection is exactly the sum of the individual
advections. No interaction force has been omitted.

For Lean, local-germ transfer is the shorter route: the existing
residual_viscosity_congr and spatialDivergence_congr can be applied after
the new finite disjoint-germ lemma, avoiding a large nonlinear sum expansion.

F07. Force admission and initial conditions.
--------------------------------------------
Each f_j is globally smooth with compact support in a positive finite time
interval and in K_j. Their finite sum has the same properties. Since the
source force's time support is separated from zero and t_j>0, so is F_R's.

The velocity and pressure vanish on a common positive initial interval:
take the minimum over finitely many activation/rest times. Therefore the
equation extends to t=0 as well: both residual and force vanish there.
This discharges the closed-time-boundary formulation without differentiating
an arbitrary negative-time extension.

F08. Energy.
-------------
Supports are disjoint. At each t<1,
  integral_R3 |U_R(t,x)|^2 dx
    =sum_j lambda_j^(-1)*integral_R3 |u_nu(s_j(t),y)|^2 dy.
For s_j(t)<0 the corresponding summand is zero. Otherwise s_j(t)<1 and
the seed uniform bound applies. Every integral is finite, and
  sup_{0<=t<1} ||U_R(t)||_2^2
    <= (sum_j lambda_j^(-1))*sup_{0<=s<1} ||u_nu(s)||_2^2 < infinity.
Exact additivity is available; a weaker finite-sum bound would already
suffice for the root theorem.

F09. Local growth at each site.
--------------------------------
Let (s_n,y_n) tend to (1,0) along the recentered physical-viscosity seed's
growth sequence. For copy j put
  t_(j,n)=1+(s_n-1)/lambda_j^2,
  x_(j,n)=a_j+y_n/lambda_j.
Then (t_(j,n),x_(j,n)) tends to (1,a_j), and
  |U_R(t_(j,n),x_(j,n))|
     =lambda_j*|u_nu(s_n,y_n)| -> infinity.
The equality holds because this point belongs to the j-th carrier when
the seed value is nonzero and the other carriers are disjoint. This proves
the prescribed local blowup property before taking the quotient.

D10. Generic-radius periodization.
-----------------------------------
First center the cube:
  U_0(t,z)=U_R(t,z+b), P_0(t,z)=P_R(t,z+b), F_0(t,z)=F_R(t,z+b).
Their supports lie in [-r,r]^3 with r<1/2. Define the velocity and pressure
to be zero for t>=1 solely as total functions used in the following lattice
sum; no smoothness or PDE across t=1 is asserted for these two extensions.
The force retains its actual smooth all-time definition.

Now put
  U_per(t,z)=sum_{k in Z^3} U_0(t,z+k),      t<1,
  P_per(t,z)=sum_{k in Z^3} P_0(t,z+k),      t<1,
  F_per(t,z)=sum_{k in Z^3} F_0(t,z+k),      all t.
The sums are locally finite because the supports are bounded. Moreover the
different translated spatial carriers are disjoint and separated by a gap.
Locally each sum equals a single translated field, or zero. Therefore all
three fields have unit periods, smoothness on the asserted domains, and
the exact periodic NSE_nu. Pressure is periodized along with velocity.

On a fundamental cube, the energy of U_per equals the whole-space energy
of U_0. Thus F08 supplies the required uniform torus energy bound. The force
is smooth and has compact positive time support. Each mixed derivative is
bounded on the compact torus times that support; multiplication by any
(1+t)^k is bounded there, proving the required rapid time decay.

Finally translate back:
  U(t,[x])=U_per(t,x-b),
  P_press(t,[x])=P_per(t,x-b), F(t,[x])=F_per(t,x-b).
Unit periods make these definitions independent of the representative x.
They solve the same equation and send a_j-b back to c(j). F09 therefore
proves LocalBlowup(U,c(j),1) for every j.

Formal interface boundary: the existing of_compact_candidate packages only
supports bounded by 1/4 in each coordinate. It must not be applied directly
to arbitrary sites across a fundamental cube. Reuse the generic r<1/2
periodization lemmas and build the corresponding generic-radius packaging
adapter. Translation back is covered by the real-translation adapter F03.

D11. Excluding a global smooth periodic competitor.
----------------------------------------------------
Suppose V,Q were a GlobalPeriodicCompetitor(nu,F). Fix 0<S<1 and let w=V-U.
On the compact torus the two smooth solutions have bounded derivatives on
[0,S], and the common force cancels. Their difference equation is
  w_t+(V dot grad)w+(w dot grad)U-nu*Delta w+grad(Q-P_press)=0,
  div w=div V=0, w(0)=0.
Pairing with w and integrating over the torus yields
  (1/2)*d/dt ||w||_2^2+nu*||grad w||_2^2
    =-integral (w dot grad)U dot w
    <= ||grad U||_infinity*||w||_2^2.
Transport and pressure integrate to zero by periodicity and divergence.
Gronwall gives w=0 on [0,S]. Since S<1 was arbitrary, V=U for all t<1.

A smooth V is bounded on a compact spacetime neighborhood of (c(j),1);
F09 contradicts this boundedness. Thus no competitor exists.
No global energy hypothesis was used for V. This is exactly the periodic
competitor scope, not the whole-space bounded-energy comparison class.

For formal reuse, the centered generic-radius CandidateProperties package
can invoke PeriodicPaper.CandidateProperties.no_global_solution directly.
A competitor for the final translated force would translate back to one
for that centered force. Alternatively the displayed periodic difference
argument is the same generic source endpoint applied after translation.

D12. Root assembly.
--------------------
Start with arbitrary nu>0, N>=1 and injective c. F01-F04 provide the seed,
local point, physical viscosity, representatives, and admissible scales.
F05-F07 give smoothness, exact PDE and force admission; F08 gives bounded
preterminal energy; F09-D10 give every prescribed local singular site and
periodicity of velocity, pressure and force; D11 excludes all competitors.
These are (D.a)-(D.g), with the original quantifiers arbitrary. Therefore
Fefferman_D_Family_obstruction follows as a B-tier corollary of the seed.

X13. Literal Fefferman D.
-------------------------
Take N=1 and any site. The zero initial datum is smooth and divergence-free.
AdmissiblePeriodicForce supplies the force condition and derivative decay.
The periodic lifts of velocity and pressure satisfy the pressure-periodicity
erratum. D11 excludes every global smooth periodic solution with the same
force and initial datum. Hence the classical alternative (D) follows from
the family endpoint. This is inheritance from the literature seed, not an
independent rediscovery of the published seed theorem.
