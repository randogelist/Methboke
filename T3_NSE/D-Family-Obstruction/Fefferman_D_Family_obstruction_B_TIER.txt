2. DEFINITIONS AND EXACT ENDPOINT
================================
Let T3=R3/Z3 carry its flat quotient metric d_T and normalized Haar measure.
The fundamental cell has volume one. Write fields with time first.

NSE_nu(U,P,F) means
  partial_t U+(U dot grad)U-nu*Delta U+grad P=F,
  div U=0,
where nu>0. Velocity and force are vector-valued; pressure is scalar.
All derivatives are the actual classical operators. At t=0 the equation uses
the right time derivative, as in the direct endpoint's literal audit.

E_T(U,t) := integral_T3 |U(t,x)|^2 dx.
E_R(u,s) := integral_R3 |u(s,y)|^2 dy.
Integrability is always established before these integrals are used. The energy
here is the SQUARED L2 norm. The upstream kineticEnergy is one half of this.

LocalBlowup(U,c,1) means:
  for every M>0, epsilon>0, delta>0 there exist t and x with
    0<t<1, 1-t<delta, d_T(x,c)<epsilon, |U(t,x)|>M.
It is local unboundedness in every left spacetime neighborhood of (1,c).
It does not assert blowup along the fixed spatial line x=c, nor that c is
isolated among all singular points.

AdmissiblePeriodicForce(F) means:
  F is globally C-infinity on R x T3, and
  for some 0<a<b<infinity, F(t,x)=0 whenever t is outside [a,b].
Consequently every mixed derivative obeys every polynomial time-decay bound
on t>=0. This includes all real decay exponents in the direct Lean contract.
A nonzero periodic lift is not compactly supported in R3.

GlobalPeriodicCompetitor(nu,F) is a pair V,Q which is C-infinity on
[0,infinity) x T3, solves the same NSE_nu with the SAME F, and has V(0)=0.
Both velocity and pressure are periodic as lifts. No support restriction,
pressure normalization, uniform all-time energy bound, smallness condition,
or energy inequality is imposed on competitors.

THEOREM: Fefferman_D_Family_obstruction
For every nu>0, integer N>=1, and injective c:Fin N -> T3, there exist F,U,P
such that:
  D.a  AdmissiblePeriodicForce(F).
  D.b  U,P are C-infinity on [0,1) x T3.
  D.c  The lifts of U,P,F are unit-periodic in each spatial coordinate.
  D.d  NSE_nu(U,P,F) holds for 0<=t<1 and U(0)=0.
  D.e  Every preterminal slice is square-integrable and sup_(0<=t<1) E_T(U,t)<infinity.
  D.f  For every j:Fin N, LocalBlowup(U,c(j),1).
  D.g  There is no GlobalPeriodicCompetitor(nu,F).

QUANTITATIVE STRENGTHENING
For arbitrary additional eta,zeta>0, the same existential witnesses can be
chosen with sup E_T(U,t)<eta and integral_0^infinity ||F(t)||_2 dt<zeta.
This implies the exact frozen family endpoint and does not change its name.
The stronger, fully quantified spatial/time/dissipation refinement is in Section 11.

QUANTIFIERS AND EXCLUSIONS
One fixed nu, N and site tuple determine one common force F for the family.
The force may change when nu, the tuple, or the budgets change.
The theorem gives at least the N requested singular sites at the common time 1.
It does not claim exactly N sites, arbitrary infinite-copy assembly, independent
singular times, or inequivalence of all family members modulo symmetries.

F01. COMMON CARRIER AND FINITE CONSTANTS
Set K_* = K union pi_x(tsupport f). It is compact because projections preserve
compactness. It contains every preterminal velocity and pressure support and
EVERY time slice of the force, including times after t=1.

Compact force support contained in the open half-space t>0 permits numbers
0<alpha<beta<infinity such that f=0 outside [alpha,beta] in time. If the support
were empty any such slab would suffice; the subsequent construction uses only
the existence of a slab. For s<0 the force is zero.
Choose B_nu>0 with E_R(u,s)<=B_nu for 0<=s<1. If the source kinetic bound is E,
choose B_nu=2E+1. Also let
  A_nu := integral_0^infinity ||f(s)||_(L2(R3)) ds < infinity.
Finiteness follows from smoothness and compact spacetime support: support lies
in a fixed compact spatial set and a finite time slab, and f is uniformly bounded.

F02. LOCAL POINT AND RECENTERING
For n>=1 choose 0<t_n<1 with 1-t_n<1/n and |u(t_n,x_n)|>n.
Then x_n belongs to K. Compactness gives a subsequence x_(n_k)->x_* in K.
Its times tend to 1 and its velocity magnitudes tend to infinity. Thus u is
locally unbounded at (1,x_*).

Replace u,p,f by their spatial translates (u,p,f)(s,y+x_*). Translate K_* too.
The new local blowup point is zero. Translation preserves nu, the exact PDE,
smoothness, energy, A_nu and the positive force-support slab.
Choose R_nu>0 with the translated K_* contained in closedBall(0,R_nu).

For s<0 redefine u(s,y)=p(s,y)=0. The source initial rest makes this replacement
C-infinity across s=0, with all jets zero there. The fields are now smooth on

at negative times and across zero. At zero, f(0)=0 by its positive-time support.
For s<0 set E_R(u,s)=0. No extension of smoothness across s=1 is claimed.

5. COMPLETE DELAYED-COPY ADMISSION
==================================
F03. Fix lambda>1 and define
  s_lambda(t)=1+lambda^2*(t-1),
  a_lambda=1-lambda^(-2),
  v_lambda(t,x)=lambda*u(s_lambda(t),lambda*x),       t<1,
  q_lambda(t,x)=lambda^2*p(s_lambda(t),lambda*x),    t<1,
  g_lambda(t,x)=lambda^3*f(s_lambda(t),lambda*x),    every real t.

The chain rule gives, evaluated at (s_lambda(t),lambda*x),
  partial_t v_lambda = lambda^3*partial_s u,
  (v_lambda dot grad)v_lambda = lambda^3*(u dot grad)u,
  Delta v_lambda = lambda^3*Delta u,
  grad q_lambda = lambda^3*grad p,
  div v_lambda = lambda^2*div u.
Therefore
  residual_nu(v_lambda,q_lambda)=lambda^3*residual_nu(u,p)=g_lambda,
  div v_lambda=0.
The viscosity is unchanged. This is a full PDE transport statement, stronger
than the already available geometric transport of LocalBlowup alone.

The spatial carrier is lambda^(-1)K_* for v_lambda,q_lambda at t<1 and for
g_lambda at ALL times. Its radius is at most R_nu/lambda.

The force-support times lie in
  [a'_lambda,b'_lambda]
    = [1+(alpha-1)/lambda^2, 1+(beta-1)/lambda^2].
Here a'_lambda>1-lambda^(-2)>0 and a'_lambda<b'_lambda<infinity.
Thus g_lambda is globally smooth with compact positive spacetime support.
No assumption that the force vanishes at or immediately after t=1 is made.

For t<=a_lambda, s_lambda(t)<=0, so v_lambda=q_lambda=0.
In particular the initial datum is zero on an open time interval around t=0.
The original initial rest supplies smoothness through the activation time.
The NSE therefore holds at t=0 with the required right derivative as well.

The exact spatial norm laws are
  E_R(v_lambda,t)=lambda^(-1)*E_R(u,s_lambda(t)),       t<1,
  ||g_lambda(t)||_(L2(R3))=lambda^(3/2)*||f(s_lambda(t))||_2,
  integral_0^infinity ||g_lambda(t)||_2 dt=lambda^(-1/2)*A_nu.
The first two follow by y=lambda*x, dx=lambda^(-3)dy. For the third,
ds=lambda^2 dt and s_lambda(0)=1-lambda^2<0; f vanishes on the extra negative
part of the source-time integration interval.

For finite spatial multi-index gamma and time derivative order m,
  partial_t^m partial_x^gamma g_lambda
    =lambda^(3+2m+|gamma|)*(partial_s^m partial_y^gamma f)(s_lambda,lambda*x).
Every derivative is smooth and bounded for a fixed lambda, although the bound
may grow rapidly as lambda increases.

For a source growth sequence (s_n,y_n)->(1,0), define
  t_n^lambda=1+(s_n-1)/lambda^2, x_n^lambda=y_n/lambda.
Then (t_n^lambda,x_n^lambda)->(1,0) and
  |v_lambda(t_n^lambda,x_n^lambda)|=lambda*|u(s_n,y_n)| -> infinity.
Thus the exact compressed seed is locally unbounded at (1,0).

6. FINITE PLACEMENT, PERIODIZATION, AND EXACT ASSEMBLY
====================================================
F04. SEPARATED TORUS CARRIERS
For N>1 set delta=min_(i!=j) d_T(c_i,c_j)>0 and
  epsilon=min(1/8,delta/4).
For N=1 set epsilon=1/8; no minimum over an empty set is used.
Define C_j=closedBall_T3(c_j,epsilon). For distinct i,j,
  2*epsilon <= delta/2 < delta,
so the closed carriers are disjoint, with a positive gap.

Choose independent lambda_j>max(1,R_nu/epsilon), or one common lambda with
this bound. Every compressed origin-centered carrier lies strictly inside
Ball_R3(0,epsilon), hence inside the central quarter cube and the interior
of the usual half-unit fundamental cube.

D10. PERIODIZE EACH ORIGIN-CENTERED COPY FIRST
For t<1 define
  vbar_j(t,[x])=sum_(k in Z3) v_(lambda_j)(t,x+k),
  qbar_j(t,[x])=sum_(k in Z3) q_(lambda_j)(t,x+k),
and for all t define
  gbar_j(t,[x])=sum_(k in Z3) g_(lambda_j)(t,x+k).
The bounded source support makes these lattice sums locally finite. Distinct
lattice copies have a positive spatial gap because their supports are inside
the quarter cube. Locally each sum equals one translated source field or zero.
Consequently the exact PDE, divergence, all asserted smoothness and unit periods
are preserved, including for the pressure.

For Lean's total functions, set v_lambda and q_lambda to zero for t>=1 solely
before forming the lattice sum. No PDE or regularity across t=1 follows from
this convention. The globally smooth force keeps its actual all-time values.

Existing source interface:
  NavierStokes.PeriodicPaper.of_compact_candidate
applies separately to each compact compressed source. The carrier K used for
that application can be enlarged to lambda_j^(-1)K_*. Its extra force-support
and local-blowup properties are retained as side facts, since the output record
does not package all of those stronger facts or the energy estimate.

In the central fundamental cube, periodization agrees with the compressed field:
all other lattice translates vanish. The same equality holds on a neighborhood
of zero. Thus local blowup at zero and the whole-space-to-cell L2 identity persist.

F05. TRANSLATE AND SUM ON THE TORUS
Put
  U_j(t,x)=vbar_j(t,x-c_j),
  P_j(t,x)=qbar_j(t,x-c_j),
  F_j(t,x)=gbar_j(t,x-c_j),
and
  U=sum_(j<N) U_j, P=sum_(j<N) P_j, F=sum_(j<N) F_j.
Torus translation is a smooth measure-preserving isometry. It preserves the
constant-coefficient NSE, viscosity, norms and positive-time force support.
Every spatial support of each triple lies in its fixed closed carrier C_j.
The all-time force carrier is included; it is not inferred merely from the
preterminal velocity support.

On R3, use closed periodic lift carriers
  Ctilde_j={x : d_T([x],c_j)<=epsilon}.
They are closed and pairwise disjoint. They need not be compact, and no
compactness of these periodic lifts is used in the finite-germ assembly.

At a given point at most one carrier is active. All other closed carriers
have a neighborhood on which their fields vanish for all time. If none is
active, take the intersection of the finitely many such neighborhoods.
Thus the sum agrees locally with one triple or with the zero triple.

F06. NONLINEAR PDE AND SMOOTHNESS
Finite sums preserve the asserted joint smoothness and periodicity.
The local equality of germs transfers time derivatives, spatial derivatives,
advection and divergence. Hence
  partial_t U+(U dot grad)U-nu*Delta U+grad P=F,
  div U=0, U(0)=0,
throughout 0<=t<1.
Equivalently, for i!=j the cross term (U_i dot grad)U_j vanishes: outside C_j
the derivative of U_j is zero, and on C_j the field U_i is zero. Therefore
  (U dot grad)U=sum_j (U_j dot grad)U_j.
There is no omitted nonlinear interaction force.

The fields vanish on a common positive initial interval because the family
is finite and every lambda_j>1. This also establishes the right-boundary
PDE at zero without making any claim at the terminal time.

F07. FORCE ADMISSION
Let a=min_j a'_(lambda_j)>0 and b=max_j b'_(lambda_j)<infinity.
The global force F is smooth, periodic, and zero outside [a,b].
Each derivative is bounded on the compact time slab times T3. For any real K,
its product with (1+t)^K is bounded on [a,b]. This proves the literal arbitrary
polynomial decay condition, with constants depending on the finite family.
It proves AdmissiblePeriodicForce(F) and the direct-D force admission.

7. ENERGY, SMALL BUDGETS, AND EVERY PRESCRIBED SINGULAR SITE
==========================================================
F08. EXACT ENERGY AND FORCE NORMS
Use normalized torus measure. No two source lattice copies overlap, and the
periodized origin-centered field equals its source on the central cell.
Translation preserves the integral. The carriers C_j are disjoint, so at each
point the squared norm of the sum equals the sum of the squared norms.
Consequently, for independent scales,
  E_T(U,t)=sum_j lambda_j^(-1)*E_R(u,s_(lambda_j)(t)),
  sup_(0<=t<1) E_T(U,t)<=B_nu*sum_j lambda_j^(-1),
  ||F(t)||_2^2=sum_j lambda_j^3*||f(s_(lambda_j)(t))||_(L2(R3))^2.
Every integral is finite; the velocity estimates apply before one and the
force identity applies for all real times.

For ONE COMMON SCALE lambda these simplify to exact identities:
  E_T(U,t)=(N/lambda)*E_R(u,s_lambda(t)),
  ||F(t)||_2=sqrt(N)*lambda^(3/2)*||f(s_lambda(t))||_(L2(R3)),
  integral_0^infinity ||F(t)||_2 dt=sqrt(N/lambda)*A_nu.
The square-root N factor in the force identity uses disjointness; it is not
an estimate obtained by discarding the nonlinear cross terms.

Given eta,zeta>0 choose
  lambda > max{1, R_nu/epsilon, N*B_nu/eta, N*A_nu^2/zeta^2}.
Then the exact same constructed family satisfies
  sup_(0<=t<1) E_T(U,t)<eta,
  integral_0^infinity ||F(t)||_2 dt<zeta.
If A_nu=0 the force estimate is still valid directly. No division by A_nu is used.
The force can have large higher derivatives and large instantaneous amplitude;
the scaling above does not bound them uniformly in lambda.

F09. PRESCRIBED LOCAL BLOWUP
Take the recentered source growth sequence (s_n,y_n)->(1,0).
For each j put
  t_(j,n)=1+(s_n-1)/lambda_j^2,
  x_(j,n)=c_j+[y_n/lambda_j] in T3.
Then t_(j,n)->1 from below and d_T(x_(j,n),c_j)->0.
For all sufficiently large n the central periodization agrees with the source
near y_n/lambda_j. Since the velocity there is nonzero, that point is in C_j;
all other family summands vanish. Therefore
  |U(t_(j,n),x_(j,n))|=lambda_j*|u(s_n,y_n)| -> infinity.
This proves LocalBlowup(U,c_j,1) for every j, at the common terminal time 1.
With a common scale the same sequence of times works for all j simultaneously.

8. SAME-FORCE COMPARISON AND ROOT CLOSURE
========================================
D11. Suppose a GlobalPeriodicCompetitor(nu,F) existed, with fields V,Q.
Fix 0<S<1 and put w=V-U. The initial data and the force agree exactly.
Subtracting the two NSE equations gives
  partial_t w+(V dot grad)w+(w dot grad)U-nu*Delta w+grad(Q-P)=0,
  div w=0, w(0)=0.
Pair with w and integrate on T3. Periodicity and incompressibility remove
transport and pressure terms. Integration by parts in diffusion gives
  (1/2)*d/dt ||w||_2^2+nu*||grad w||_2^2
    =-integral_T3 ((w dot grad)U) dot w
    <= ||grad U||_infinity*||w||_2^2.
The coefficient is bounded on [0,S] because U is smooth there. Gronwall yields
w=0 on [0,S]. Since S<1 was arbitrary, V=U for every t<1.
Smoothness of V bounds it on the compact cylinder [0,1] x T3, contradicting
LocalBlowup at any one prescribed site. N>=1 ensures that such a site exists.
Thus no GlobalPeriodicCompetitor(nu,F) exists.

D12. For arbitrary nu,N,c and optional budgets, choose the stronger seed, recenter,

F07 proves D.a; D10/F05/F06 prove D.b-D.d; F08 proves D.e and the quantitative
budgets; F09 proves D.f; D11 proves D.g. Every quantified choice has been made.

X13. Taking N=1 gives smooth periodic divergence-free datum u0=0, an admissible

11.2 ENERGY AND DISSIPATION OF THE SAME SEED
Take exactly the initial-rest seed in Section 4. Define
  a(t):=||f(t)||_(L2(R3)),
  H(t):=integral_0^t a(s) ds,          0<=t<=1,
  A:=integral_0^infinity a(s) ds,
  M:=sup_(0<=t<1) ||u(t)||_(L2(R3)),
  D^2:=integral_0^1 ||grad u(t)||_(L2(R3))^2 dt.
Smooth compact spacetime support of f gives A<infinity. The source theorem
candidate_energy_estimates applies to THIS candidate and establishes actual
dissipation integrability, not only a totalized-integral expression.

For completeness, the analytic derivation is:
  (1/2)*(d/dt)||u(t)||_2^2+nu*||grad u(t)||_2^2
    =integral_R3 f(t,x) dot u(t,x) dx
    <=a(t)*||u(t)||_2.
All integrations by parts are justified on every shorter time interval by
smoothness and compact spatial support. Regularize the square root of the
energy by sqrt(||u||_2^2+epsilon), integrate, and let epsilon decrease to zero.
Zero datum then yields ||u(t)||_2<=H(t). Reinserting this bound in the
integrated energy identity gives
  ||u(t)||_2^2+2*nu*integral_0^t ||grad u(s)||_2^2 ds
    <=2*integral_0^t a(s)*H(s) ds=H(t)^2.
Nonnegativity and monotone convergence as t increases to one prove
  M<=A,                 sqrt(2*nu)*D<=A.
Spatial recentering leaves all these norms unchanged. The zero extension to
negative source times contributes zero to every norm and dissipation integral.

11.3 EXACT SIMULTANEOUS SCALING
Use one common lambda>1 for all N copies and the same
  s_lambda(t)=1+lambda^2*(t-1)
as in Section 5. Before translation/periodization the amplitude factors are
  velocity: lambda; gradient: lambda^2; force: lambda^3.
Spatial volume contributes lambda^(-3); time contributes lambda^(-2).
Thus, for the assembled disjoint family,
  ||U||_(L-infinity_t L2_x)=sqrt(N/lambda)*M,
  ||grad U||_(L2_t L2_x)=sqrt(N/lambda)*D,
  ||F||_(L1_t L2_x)=sqrt(N/lambda)*A.
The gradient identity uses the fact that a smooth field vanishes with all its
derivatives off its closed carrier. Hence gradient cross products also vanish.
The source time range (0,1) is fully traversed; the extra negative-time part is zero.

For any 1<=p,q<=infinity, put A_pq:=||f||_(Lq((0,infinity);Lp(R3))).
These constants are finite because the force is smooth and compactly supported.
Disjoint supports give, for finite p, the sum of N identical p-th powers at
each time. For p=infinity the norm is the maximum over the disjoint copies.
The common time reparametrization therefore gives the EXACT identity
  ||F||_(Lq_t Lp_x)=N^(1/p)*lambda^(3-3/p-2/q)*A_pq.
For q=infinity the time factor is one; otherwise it follows by change of variables.
All force support lies at positive target times, so the time change loses none
of the source support, including its part after source time one.

For sigma=3/p+2/q-3>0 this is N^(1/p)*lambda^(-sigma)*A_pq.
Consequently any finite list of positive-sigma budgets can be met together.
With p,q>=1, positive sigma automatically excludes p=infinity or q=infinity;
the displayed identity itself remains valid at those endpoints.

13. ZERO-FORCE SPECIALIZATION: FIRST OBSTRUCTION
===============================================
Question: can one choose f=0 and retain LocalBlowup for the present family?
Scope: the same viscosity nu>0, classical periodic velocity and pressure,
zero initial datum, and the finite localized witnesses of Sections 2-11.

13.1 ZERO INITIAL DATA RULE OUT IDENTICALLY ZERO FORCE
On every shorter interval [0,t], t<1, smoothness and periodicity give
  (1/2)*||U(t)||_2^2+nu*integral_0^t ||grad U(r)||_2^2 dr
    =integral_0^t integral_T3 f(r,x) dot U(r,x) dx dr.
If f=0 on [0,1), the right side vanishes. Both terms on the left are
nonnegative, hence U(t)=0 for every t<1. Smoothness upgrades equality
almost everywhere in space to pointwise equality. LocalBlowup is impossible.

Thus the proposed implication
  current zero-data family + f=0 -> a blowup witness
is BLOCKED, with an actual contradiction rather than a missing estimate.
The same conclusion holds if f=grad(phi) throughout [0,1), for a smooth
globally periodic scalar phi: incompressibility makes its energy work zero,
and replacing P by P-phi removes the force. This includes either sign by
changing phi. Periodicity of the scalar potential is essential.

The proof uses no hypothesis about the force after the singular time.
It is not a claim about arbitrary nonzero initial data.

13.2 SMALL FORCE, FINITE CANCELLATION AND THE ZERO LIMIT
In the common-scale construction,
  ||F_lambda||_(L1_t L2_x)=sqrt(N/lambda)*A.
The nontrivial zero-data seed must have A>0, by the preceding energy argument.
Consequently this norm is nonzero for every finite lambda and N>=1.
Arbitrarily small forcing is therefore not an identically zero-force case.
Also, the disjoint force carriers prevent cancellation: if sum_j F_j=0,
then restricting to each carrier gives F_j=0 separately.

As lambda tends to infinity, the quantitative estimates give U_lambda->0
in L-infinity((0,1);L2) and in the dissipation norm, as well as F_lambda->0
in L1_t L2_x. The energy-space limit is the zero trajectory. LocalBlowup
does not pass to this limit. No unforced blowup witness is obtained by it.

13.3 THE EXISTING TERMINAL-INTERVAL OBSTRUCTION APPLIES TO THIS FAMILY
This is the specialization of Sections 7-8 of the pressure-force gauge
companion to the spatial carriers already constructed here.

Let Pi denote the Leray projection with the constant spatial mode removed,
and define, for 0<=s<1,
  J_F(s):=integral_s^1 ||Pi F(t)||_2^2 dt.
For the present family,
  mean U(t)=0 for 0<=t<1,
  mean F(t)=0 for 0<t<1.
Indeed, for each compact solenoidal whole-space copy,
  integral_R3 u_i=integral_R3 div(x_i*u)=0;
periodization and torus translation preserve this integral. The periodic
momentum equation then gives the preterminal mean-force identity.
No mean-force assertion after time one is needed or made.

The velocity vanishes on one fixed nonempty spatial open set O for all t<1.
To see this, its support is contained in a finite union of disjoint closed
small balls. One small ball cannot fill the torus. For two or more, such a
finite disjoint closed covering would disconnect the connected torus.
Thus the complement of their union is nonempty and open.

Suppose J_F(s)=0. Smoothness of F and L2-continuity of Pi imply Pi F=0
throughout (s,1). Together with mean F=0, the torus Hodge decomposition
gives F=grad(phi) with a smooth periodic potential on this interval.
Replacing P by P-phi makes the SAME U solve unforced periodic NSE there.

Positive-time spatial analyticity of smooth unforced periodic NSE implies
that each slice U(t,.) for s<t<1 is real analytic. This follows locally
after restarting before t and using smooth uniqueness. Since that analytic
slice vanishes on O, the identity theorem on connected T3 gives U(t,.)=0.
This contradicts its terminal local blowup. Therefore
  for EVERY 0<=s<1,       0<J_F(s)<infinity.
In particular F cannot vanish, or be a pure periodic gradient, on any
terminal interval (s,1) while preserving this localized trajectory.

This does NOT assert F(1) is nonzero, a positive uniform lower bound on J_F(s),
or nonzero force at the singular point itself. Smoothness implies J_F(s)->0
as s increases to one. The assertion concerns every whole terminal interval.
