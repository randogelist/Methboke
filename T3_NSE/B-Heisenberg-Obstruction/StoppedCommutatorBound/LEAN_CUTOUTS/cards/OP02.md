```lean
/-- The graph of a `LinearPMap` viewed as a submodule on `E × F`. -/
def graph [Module R F] (f : E →ₗ.[R] F) : Submodule R (E × F) :=
  f.toFun.graph.map (f.domain.subtype.prodMap (LinearMap.id : F →ₗ[R] F))
```
