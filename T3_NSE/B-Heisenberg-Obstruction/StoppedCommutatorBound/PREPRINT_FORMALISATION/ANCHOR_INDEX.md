## Normalization and new work

- `coefficientDissipation` is the full squared gradient norm; the present enstrophy observable is half of it.
- `W_N = - coefficientEnstrophyNonlinearPower`; `Z_N = coefficientLaplacianSquare`. This sign and the factor two must survive every adapter.
- The complete two-sided flow, L2 half-density representation and self-adjoint/core realization are new formal obligations.
- A normalized smooth bump density is not automatically an L2-normalized packet. Scale a nonzero smooth bump by its L2 norm.
- `SOBOLEV_DIVERGENCE_PRODUCT_NORM_BOUND` has its literal derivative indices. It does not by itself prove the H2×H3 product estimate or the E09 bootstrap.
- Existing uniform local Galerkin estimates and estimates conditional on a uniform H2 integral are not the E09 smooth-solution stability theorem.
- The proposed existential stopped bound is not proved by any indexed declaration.

## Operator-source search boundary

- No verified pinned declaration for Stone's theorem / self-adjoint generator of an arbitrary strongly continuous unitary group was located. Search failure is not an absence proof.
- Mathlib/Analysis/InnerProductSpace/Symmetric.lean defines symmetry for everywhere-defined linear maps; its Hellinger–Toeplitz continuity theorem cannot be substituted for essential self-adjointness of the unbounded phase-space differential operator.
- Complete smooth finite-dimensional flow, Jacobian differential equation, L2 half-density group construction, strong continuity, unbounded-generator domain/core, and exact Hilbert-space Ehrenfest identity require explicit construction or separately located and audited endpoints.
